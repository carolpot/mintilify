---
title: "Export Company Data to CSV"
description: "How to export company data from Lusha Search to a CSV file, including bulk export options for Scale and Enterprise plans."
---

If you're on a **Scale** or **Enterprise** plan, you can export company data from the classic Lusha Prospecting search into a CSV file for use in your external workflows.

<Note>
If you are working in a **Workspace search or filter view**, you cannot export companies directly. Save your search results as a Workspace table first, then export from the table. See [Exporting from a Workspace Table](/user-guide/managing-and-exporting-data/exporting-from-a-workspace-table).
</Note>

## Export up to 25 companies at a time

1. On the **Search** tab, run a [Company search](https://dashboard.lusha.com/prospecting/companies).
2. Select individual companies or click the checkbox at the top to select up to **25 companies**.
3. Click **Export CSV**.

A CSV file with the selected company data will be downloaded to your computer.

## Export more than 25 companies

If you need to export more than 25 companies, you have two options:

### 1. Use the Intent or Technology filter (up to 10K companies)

1. Start a [Company search](https://dashboard.lusha.com/prospecting/companies) and include **at least one value** from the **Intent** or **Technology** filter.
2. Ensure no contacts are selected - this activates the **Export 10K** button.
3. Click **Export CSV**.

The CSV file will be sent directly to your email.

This export option is available once per hour.
</Note>

### 2. Exporting from a Saved Company Table

1. Save companies to your table in batches of 25.
2. Open the table and click **CSV** (do not select any specific companies).

All companies in the table will be downloaded as a CSV file.
