</Info>

## What Lusha does

Lusha is a B2B data intelligence platform that helps revenue teams find the right people, keep records accurate, and act on real-world changes across their go-to-market systems. It addresses common problems like missing contact details, outdated job titles, high email bounce rates, stale prospect lists, and manual research.

Lusha serves several types of users:

- **Sales reps** (BDRs, SDRs, AEs) - build prospect tables, reveal verified emails and direct dials, and sync contacts to a CRM.
- **RevOps and GTM ops** - keep CRM records clean, automate enrichment, and improve lead routing.
- **Marketers and demand gen** - enrich leads, reduce bounce rates, and power ABM campaigns.
- **Recruiters** - extract contact details from LinkedIn and validate company information.
