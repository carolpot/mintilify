<Warning>
This article describes the previous experience. Activity-Based Recommendations have been merged into the unified AI Recommendations engine, powered by ICP Hub. The workflows, widgets, and UI described below no longer exist as standalone features. For the current experience, see [ICP Hub: How AI Recommendations Work](/user-guide/ai-recommendations/icp-hub-setup-scoring-and-recommendations).
</Warning>

**What this was:** Activity-Based Recommendations surfaced AI-generated lookalike contacts and companies based on your last 90 days of prospecting activity (or your last 1,500 revealed contacts). Tables refreshed daily and appeared on the homepage and in the Search dashboard.

In the current ICP Hub, your prospecting history is one of the signals that feeds into ICP Fit Score and Signal Score - the same underlying intent, just unified across all recommendation types and accessible from a single hub.
