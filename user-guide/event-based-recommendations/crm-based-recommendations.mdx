CRM-based recommendations are now part of the unified AI Recommendations engine, powered by ICP Hub. CRM data is used both as a context signal for scoring and for exclusion rules. The information below reflects the previous standalone experience. To learn how the current system works, see the [ICP Hub: How AI Recommendations Work](/user-guide/ai-recommendations/icp-hub-setup-scoring-and-recommendations) guide.

## Overview

CRM-based recommendations use your existing CRM data to surface new, high-quality leads that resemble the companies and contacts you already engage with. Once your CRM is connected, Lusha's AI analyzes your past opportunities to deliver a personalized feed of companies and decision-makers that align with your sales motion.

These recommendations appear in the new Home Screen experience and are refreshed daily to keep your pipeline filled with fresh, relevant prospects.

---

## Use cases

- **Find lookalike leads based on your pipeline** — Identify companies and contacts similar to those you already engage with to expand into high-fit accounts faster.
- **Accelerate outbound table building** — Get a daily stream of leads that match your top opportunities, no manual searching required.
- **Scale what works** — Let AI surface leads with similar traits to your best-performing accounts, like industry, location, or role.
- **Sharpen your ABM targeting** — Fuel your account-based marketing with companies that match your ideal customer profile.

---

## How it works

Lusha's CRM-based recommendations are powered by sales data and recommend new, high-fit companies and contacts that resemble what's already working for your team.

<Note>
This feature currently supports Salesforce. HubSpot support is available in the new ICP Hub. See the [ICP Hub guide](/user-guide/ai-recommendations/icp-hub-setup-scoring-and-recommendations) for details.
</Note>

**Daily Updates**

Recommendations are refreshed daily, ensuring they remain relevant and tailored to your evolving sales goals.

## How to disable CRM-based recommendations

**Disconnect the CRM integration**

Admins can disconnect the CRM at any time. Once disconnected, Lusha will stop accessing CRM data, and CRM-based recommendations will no longer be available for any user in the account.

**Request removal via Support**

CRM-based recommendations can also be disabled for individual users upon request. The request must come from an admin and be submitted through Lusha Support.

## FAQs

**Q: What are CRM-based recommendations?**

**A:** CRM-based recommendations are an AI-powered feature that brings you companies and contacts similar to the ones already in your CRM. It helps uncover lookalike leads aligned with your sales motion, based on opportunities in your existing activity.

**Q: How does it work?**

**A:** Once your admin connects your CRM from the homepage or provides consent from the homepage. Our AI engine identifies lookalikes in the data and suggests similar high-potential leads. These recommendations update daily and require no manual effort.

**Q: What CRM platforms are supported?**

**A:** In this experience, CRM-based recommendations are available for Salesforce only. The new ICP Hub supports both Salesforce and HubSpot, with more CRMs coming soon.

**Q: How often do the recommendations update?**

**A:** They refresh daily, incorporating recent activity to ensure your lead table stays relevant and aligned with your workflow.

**Q: What data from my CRM is used?**

**A:** We use high-level opportunity data without accessing sensitive details such as revenue. When customers use Lusha's CRM integration for data enrichment, the data accessed from their CRM is strictly used for the intended enrichment process. It is not added to Lusha's database for selling purposes, nor is it sold to third parties.

**Q: Is it secure?**

**A:** Yes. Your CRM data is read securely, never shared, and never used to train any AI models. Your data stays private and under your control, and only admins can connect or disconnect your CRM. Lusha ensures that customer CRM data and its broader contact database are kept physically separated.

**Q: What if we want to disconnect later?**

**A:** Admins can disconnect CRM access at any time. Once disconnected, we stop accessing new data and CRM-based recommendations will no longer be available for any user in the account.

**Q: Will this impact GDPR or compliance policies?**

**A:** No. Lusha is fully committed to privacy and compliance. CRM data is not shared and we follow strict global standards, including GDPR. You remain the data controller at all times.
