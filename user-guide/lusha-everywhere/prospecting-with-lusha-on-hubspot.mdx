The Lusha extension works directly inside HubSpot, letting you enrich contact records and prospect by account without leaving your CRM. This means fewer tabs, cleaner data, and faster ABM outreach.

## Contact enrichment

To enrich a HubSpot contact with verified details:

1. Go to the contact's **Contact page** in HubSpot.
2. Make sure the company side panel is open.

1. Open the Lusha extension to see if contact data is available.
2. Click **Show contact** to reveal the contact's direct dial and email. This uses one credit.
3. Click **Save contact** to push the data to a Lusha table, or export it directly to Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Outreach, Salesloft, or Zoho.

## Account-based prospecting from HubSpot

To find and reach contacts at a target account:

1. Go to the company's **Company page** in HubSpot.
2. Open the Lusha extension to see a snapshot of the company and its employees.
3. Search by name or title, or filter by department, seniority, contact location, or data type to find the right person.
4. Click **Show contact** to reveal details, then email the contact directly from the extension or auto-dial from HubSpot.

You're all set — Lusha on HubSpot keeps your contact data accurate and your outreach focused, all in one place.
