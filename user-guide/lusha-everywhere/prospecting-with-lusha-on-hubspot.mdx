The Lusha extension works directly inside HubSpot, letting you enrich contact records and prospect by account without leaving your CRM. This means fewer tabs, cleaner data, and faster ABM outreach.

## Contact enrichment

To enrich a HubSpot contact with verified details:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact's Contact page">
    Go to the contact's **Contact page** in HubSpot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the company side panel">
    Make sure the company side panel is open.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://lusha-eu.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/13259744/be8f1fe48b85b36cb2814390/image-20231109-091914%2B-284-29.png?expires=1780056000&signature=28fc33ecd4f4d7127b9c256201c40bdc9dc9cc08cbe35ef37d7c3aff9241426e&req=0dZtwFH9qDNk2hj99dpg6slm4LG9Dl9D63eSfTmqaIT4enJsrzeLBX5zqDFZ%0ApjwT2tEv0BjtCMbkZm49sNT%2F%0A" alt="HubSpot contact page with the company side panel open" style={{maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Lusha extension">
    Open the Lusha extension to see if contact data is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reveal the contact">
    Click **Show contact** to reveal the contact's direct dial and email. This uses one credit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or export the contact">
    Click **Save contact** to push the data to a Lusha table, or export it directly to Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Outreach, Salesloft, or Zoho.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account-based prospecting from HubSpot

To find and reach contacts at a target account:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company's Company page">
    Go to the company's **Company page** in HubSpot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Lusha extension">
    Open the Lusha extension to see a snapshot of the company and its employees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or filter for the right person">
    Search by name or title, or filter by department, seniority, contact location, or data type to find the right person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reveal and contact them">
    Click **Show contact** to reveal details, then email the contact directly from the extension or auto-dial from HubSpot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You're all set - Lusha on HubSpot keeps your contact data accurate and your outreach focused, all in one place.
