title: "Prospecting with Lusha on Salesforce"
description: "How to use the Lusha extension in Salesforce to enrich contacts and prospect by account without leaving your CRM."
---

The Lusha extension works directly inside Salesforce, letting you enrich contact records and prospect by account without leaving your CRM.

## Contact enrichment

1. In Salesforce, go to the **Contacts** tab and open the contact record you want to enrich.
2. Click the **Lusha extension** icon to open the panel.
3. Click **Show contact** to reveal the contact's direct dial, work number, and email. This uses one credit.

1. Click **Save contact** to sync the data to a Lusha table or another platform.

## Account-based prospecting from Salesforce

1. In Salesforce, go to the **Accounts** tab and open the account record you want to prospect.
2. Click the **Lusha extension** icon to open the panel.

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/15425150/c56cea105b8fc005490f184d/1A58F3B7-4997-4366-B0D9-D760807E9D3A_1_105_c.jpeg?expires=1780056000&signature=9e2743930070c61b4c6ec15af72e14c7736d11f1b22e37445596575e86c165ea&req=0dBrx137qTdk2hj99dpg6sSp1wDQoZGPdiW6QUuKSonv%2Fk0G%2F25BQ5Ouip%2F3%0AQedMOT1hsqL8fjfII3kdtH0K%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}} />

1. Go to the **All employees** tab to see contacts from that account in Lusha's database.
2. Filter by **Title**, **Seniority**, or **Department** to narrow your targets. You can also filter by contact location or email type.
3. Click **Show contact** on any contact to reveal their direct, work phone, and email.
4. Reach out instantly — email directly from the extension or auto-dial from Salesforce.
