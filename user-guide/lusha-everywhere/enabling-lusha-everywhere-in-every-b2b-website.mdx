---
title: "Enabling Lusha Everywhere in every B2B website"
description: "How to enable Lusha Everywhere so the extension works on any B2B website, not just LinkedIn."
---

Upon installing the Lusha extension, you'll be asked to approve Lusha to run on all B2B websites. This lets Lusha surface contact data when you browse company websites directly.

While using the extension on LinkedIn, go to the **More** tab and set the **Enable Lusha everywhere** toggle to **ON**.

The next time you visit a B2B website, the Lusha extension will be available and provide contact data.

You can always control how Lusha appears on company websites by changing the toggle in the **More** tab.
