description: "How to use the Lusha extension in Gmail to prospect by company, filter by department or seniority, and reveal contact details."
---

The Lusha extension works directly inside Gmail via Lusha Everywhere, letting you enrich contacts and prospect by account without leaving your inbox.

## How to enable Lusha on Gmail

1. Open the Lusha extension.
2. Click **More**.
3. Toggle **Enable Lusha Everywhere** to **Always On**.
