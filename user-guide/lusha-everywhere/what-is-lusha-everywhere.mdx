---
title: "What is Lusha Everywhere?"
description: "How to use Lusha Everywhere to find and reveal contact details directly from any B2B company website."
---

Lusha Everywhere is a feature of the Lusha extension that lets you prospect directly from any B2B company website - not just LinkedIn. Browse to a company's website, open the extension, and instantly see a table of employees and their contact details.

To use it, make sure **Lusha Everywhere** is enabled in your extension settings. You can toggle it on from the **More → Settings** tab in the extension panel.

## How to prospect with Lusha Everywhere

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Lusha">
    Log in to your [Lusha account](https://dashboard.lusha.com/dashboard).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to a company website">
    Navigate to any company website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the extension">
    Click the **blue Lusha icon** on the right side of your browser to open the extension.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the company overview">
    Lusha will display the company overview and a table of employees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter down to your contacts">
    Use the **Department**, **Seniority**, or **Contact information** filters to narrow down the contacts you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reveal contact details">
    Click **Show details** on any contact to reveal their email address and phone numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click the star next to the company name to add it to your [Favorites on the Prospecting Platform](https://dashboard.lusha.com/prospecting/favorites). Click **View all employees** to open the full employee table in Lusha Search.

## What you can do with revealed contacts

## Save to a table

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your contacts">
    Select the contacts you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click table">
    Click **table**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or create a table">
    Choose an existing table or click **Create new table**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a tag (optional)">
    Optionally add a [tag](/user-guide/getting-started-with-prospecting/contact-tagging) for organisation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export to CRM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click CRM">
    Click **CRM** after revealing contact details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your CRM and record type">
    Select your CRM. Connected integrations appear at the top of the list under **Connected**; other available ones are shown below under **Not Connected**. Then choose the record type (Lead, Contact, etc.).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add to a sequence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Sequence">
    Click **Sequence** under the contact's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or create a sequence">
    Select an existing sequence or save a new one as a draft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can add up to 25 contacts at once to a sequence from Lusha Everywhere.

<Note>
  Contacts without email addresses cannot be added to sequences.
</Note>

Now you can find and engage prospects from any company website - not just LinkedIn.
