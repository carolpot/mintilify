title: "What is Lusha Everywhere?"
description: "How to use Lusha Everywhere to find and reveal contact details directly from any B2B company website."
---

Lusha Everywhere is a feature of the Lusha extension that lets you prospect directly from any B2B company website - not just LinkedIn. Browse to a company's website, open the extension, and instantly see a table of employees and their contact details.

To use it, make sure **Lusha Everywhere** is enabled in your extension settings. You can toggle it on from the **More → Settings** tab in the extension panel.

## How to prospect with Lusha Everywhere

1. Log in to your [Lusha account](https://dashboard.lusha.com/dashboard).
2. Navigate to any company website.
3. Click the **blue Lusha icon** on the right side of your browser to open the extension.
4. Lusha will display the company overview and a table of employees.
5. Use the **Department**, **Seniority**, or **Contact information** filters to narrow down the contacts you want.
6. Click **Show details** on any contact to reveal their email address and phone numbers.

Click the star next to the company name to add it to your [Favorites on the Prospecting Platform](https://dashboard.lusha.com/prospecting/favorites). Click **View all employees** to open the full employee table in Lusha Search.

## What you can do with revealed contacts

## Save to a table

1. Select the contacts you want.
2. Click **table**.
3. Choose an existing table or click **Create new table**.
4. Optionally add a [tag](/user-guide/getting-started-with-prospecting/contact-tagging) for organisation.
5. Click **Save**.

## Export to CRM

1. Click **CRM** after revealing contact details.
2. Select your CRM. Connected integrations appear at the top of the list under **Connected**; other available ones are shown below under **Not Connected**. Then choose the record type (Lead, Contact, etc.).

## Add to a sequence

1. Click **Sequence** under the contact's name.
2. Select an existing sequence or save a new one as a draft.

You can add up to 25 contacts at once to a sequence from Lusha Everywhere.

<Note>
  Contacts without email addresses cannot be added to sequences.
</Note>

Now you can find and engage prospects from any company website - not just LinkedIn.
