---
title: "How to Set Up Auto Top-Up for Credits"
description: "Learn how to automatically purchase credits before your balance runs low to help prevent interruptions to your team's work."
---

Automatic Credit Top-Up adds credits to your account when your balance drops to a threshold you define. You choose when a top-up is triggered, how many credits to purchase, and how many automatic purchases are allowed per billing cycle.

This article walks you through setting up Auto Top-Up, understanding your spend controls, and managing the feature over time.

## Plan availability

Automatic Credit Top-Up is available on **Starter**, **Pro**, and **Premium** plans. It is not available on Free Trial or Scale/Enterprise accounts. Only account **Admins** can configure this feature.

## Before you begin

Make sure your account has a saved payment method. Auto Top-Up cannot be enabled without one.

To add a card, go to **My Account** → **Payments & Subscription** → **Payment Method** → **Add card**.

## How to set up Auto Top-Up

1. Go to **My Account** in the top navigation bar.
2. Click the **Payments & Subscription** tab.
3. Find the **Auto top-up** section and click **Set up**.
4. Set your **Trigger Threshold** in the setup modal — the credit balance at which an automatic purchase fires.

<Note>
The minimum trigger threshold is 200 credits. It cannot be set to 0. The credits remaining above the threshold act as a buffer while payment processes, so your team experiences no interruption.
</Note>

5. Select the credit amount to purchase from the dropdown. Available bundles match Lusha's standard top-up packages. The equivalent dollar cost is calculated and displayed automatically — this field is read-only.

6. Set your **Cycle top-up limit** — the maximum number of automatic purchases allowed per billing cycle. The system displays the maximum you can select based on your plan.

   - The minimum is 1 top-up per cycle.
   - The **"Up to $X / cycle"** figure shown next to this field is the maximum you could be charged if every allowed top-up fires. This field is read-only.

<Note>
For monthly plans, the billing cycle is your monthly billing cycle date (subscription start date). For annual plans, it's the full annual period.
</Note>

7. Confirm your saved payment method is correct.
8. Click **Save & Enable**.

<Note>
Once enabled, Auto Top-Up remains active at renewal unless you actively turn it off.
</Note>

## What happens when Auto Top-Up fires

When your balance reaches the trigger threshold, Lusha checks two conditions before charging:

- Your cycle top-up limit has not been reached.
- At least 12 hours have passed since the last auto top-up (a built-in anti-fraud safeguard).

If both conditions are met, the purchase fires automatically. Credits are added to your account as soon as payment is confirmed. You'll receive:

- An email with the amount charged, credits added, and a link to manage your settings.
- An in-platform notification with a direct link to your Auto Top-Up settings.

Each auto top-up purchase generates an invoice that appears in your **Payments & Subscription** page alongside your other billing history.

## What happens when the cycle limit is reached

Once you've reached the maximum number of automatic top-ups for your billing cycle, Auto Top-Up pauses until your next billing cycle begins. You'll receive an email and an in-platform notification.

If your account runs out of credits while Auto Top-Up is paused, you'll need to purchase credits manually or upgrade your plan to continue using Lusha.

<Warning>
If your team runs out of credits while Auto Top-Up is paused, they will see the standard credit limit pop-up and won't be able to use Lusha until credits are added.
</Warning>

## How to update or disable Auto Top-Up

You can change any part of your configuration at any time:

1. Go to **My Account** → **Payments & Subscription**.
2. In the **Auto top-up** section, click **Set up** (or the edit option if already configured).
3. Update your settings and save — changes take effect immediately.

To turn it off, toggle **Auto top-up** to the off position. You'll see a confirmation: "Auto top-up has been disabled. Your team will not receive automatic credit top-ups."

## If a payment fails

If the automatic charge fails (for example, due to an expired card), no credits are added. You'll receive the standard billing failure email with a link to update your payment method.

Auto Top-Up remains enabled and will try again the next time your credit balance reaches the configured threshold.

---

You're all set! Once enabled, Automatic Credit Top-Ups run automatically based on the settings you've configured. You can update or disable them at any time.
