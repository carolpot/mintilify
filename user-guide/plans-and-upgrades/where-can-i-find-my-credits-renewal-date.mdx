---
title: "Where can I find my credits renewal date?"
description: "Where to find your Lusha credits renewal date in your account settings and dashboard."
---

Your credit renewal date tells you when your monthly or annual credit allowance resets.

<Note>
New to credits? Start with [How credits work in Lusha](/user-guide/getting-started/how-credits-work-in-lusha) for the basics - plan allowances, rollover, and reset dates.
</Note>

<Note>
The renewal date in the Payments & Subscription tab is only visible to Admins and Managers.
</Note>

## Payments & Subscription tab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account menu">
    Click your name at the bottom-left of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Payment & Subscription">
    Select **Account**, then go to **[Payments & Subscription](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account/subscription)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your renewal date">
    Your renewal date is listed under the subscription details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The sidebar only shows your current credit balance, not a renewal date. The account-menu plan card also shows your credits, but not a renewal date. The Payments & Subscription tab is the place to check it.
</Note>

You're all set - now you know exactly when your credits refresh.
