---
title: "Where can I find my credits renewal date?"
description: "Where to find your Lusha credits renewal date in your account settings and dashboard."
---

Your credit renewal date tells you when your monthly or annual credit allowance resets. You can find it in two places in Lusha.

<Note>
The renewal date in the Payments & Subscription tab is only visible to Admins and Managers.
</Note>

## Option 1: Payments & Subscription tab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account menu">
    Click your name at the bottom-left of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Payment & Subscription">
    Select **Settings**, then go to **[Payment & Subscription](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account/subscription)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your renewal date">
    Your renewal date is listed under the subscription details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Option 2: dashboard overview

On the left side of your [Lusha dashboard](https://dashboard.lusha.com/dashboard), look for the **Plan Details** section. Your renewal date is shown there alongside your current plan and credit balance.

You're all set - now you know exactly when your credits refresh.
