---
title: "How to record a live or unscheduled meeting"
description: "Start recording a meeting manually in Lusha Conversations, even if it wasn't on your calendar."
---

When a meeting wasn't scheduled in advance - or if the recording bot missed a call - you can start a recording manually using the **New Recording** button in Lusha Conversations.

<Note>
Each conversation recording costs 8 credits. For legacy Scale plans, credits are deducted from your Bulk Credits.
</Note>

## Before you start

- You need Lusha Conversations set up. See [Lusha Conversations](/user-guide/getting-started-with-conversations/lusha-conversations-overview) if you haven't set it up yet.
- The meeting must already be in progress or about to start.
- You'll need the meeting link (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, etc.).

## How to record a live meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Conversations">
    Go to **Engagement → Conversations** (/ci/meetings) in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new recording">
    Click **New Recording** in the top-right corner of your Conversations dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the meeting link">
    Paste your virtual meeting link into the field provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename the meeting (optional)">
    Optionally rename the meeting for easier organisation later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the bot join and record">
    The Lusha bot will join the meeting and begin recording automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the meeting ends, your recording will appear in the Conversations table within a few minutes and will be processed for transcript and analysis.

<Note>
All participants are automatically notified when recording begins. A notification appears in the meeting chat. Admins can customise this message in **Bot Settings**.
</Note>

## How to stop a recording in progress

- **If you are the meeting host** - remove the Lusha bot from the participant table, just like any other attendee.
- **If you are not the host** - click **Stop Recording** from the Conversations banner at the top of your screen.

<Warning>
Make sure you have consent from all participants before recording, in accordance with your local recording laws.
</Warning>

Once the recording is processed, you'll find the full transcript and analysis in your Conversations table.
