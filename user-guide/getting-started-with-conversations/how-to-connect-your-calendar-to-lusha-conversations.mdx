---
title: "How to connect your calendar to Lusha Conversations"
description: "Learn how to connect your Google or Outlook calendar so Lusha can automatically join and record your meetings."
---

Lusha Conversations records and analyzes your sales calls. To enable automatic recording, you need to connect the calendar account where your meetings are scheduled. Once connected, Lusha's recording bot joins your scheduled external meetings automatically - no manual action needed per meeting.

<Note>
Lusha uses the same Gmail or Outlook connection for both email sequences (Engage) and calendar sync (Conversations). If you've already connected your email in Engage, check whether your calendar is already linked before following these steps.
</Note>

## Before you start

- You need a Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar account with your meetings scheduled in it.
- Your Gmail or Outlook account must be authorized in Lusha (see the Salesforce integration settings).

## How to connect your calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Conversations">
    Go to **Engagement → Conversations** (/ci/meetings) in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Organization settings">
    Click the **Settings** icon (⚙️, top left) → **Organization** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Auto Join Meetings">
    Under **Auto Join Meetings**, toggle ON.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Join Non-Host Meetings (if needed)">
    If you attend meetings you did not schedule, also enable **Join Non-Host Meetings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Save your settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once connected, Conversations joins and records your scheduled external meetings automatically. After each meeting ends, you'll receive an analysis email within 2–5 minutes.

<Note>
Only **external meetings** (with participants outside your Lusha account domain) are recorded by default. You can adjust this in **Settings → Recording Preferences**.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting - meetings not being recorded

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your calendar connection">
    Verify your calendar connection is active in **Settings → Personal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your auto-recording rules">
    Check your auto-recording rules to confirm the meeting type is included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the recording toggle">
    In the **Upcoming Meetings** section, confirm the recording toggle is on for that specific meeting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You're all set! Lusha Conversations will now auto-record your external meetings.
