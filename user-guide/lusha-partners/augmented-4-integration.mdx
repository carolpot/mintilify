Augmented 4 (TA4) is an Enterprise AI Agent Platform with vertical AI agents for automated lead qualification and personalized outreach. The Lusha integration pulls verified contact data directly into TA4 sequences, so your team can move from raw lead to ready-to-close deal without manual data entry or spreadsheet work.

Once connected, Lusha-verified contacts flow automatically into TA4's AI agents, which handle qualification and outreach on your behalf.

## **What you can do with this integration**

- **Automated lead capture** - pull Lusha-verified contacts directly into Augmented 4 sequences
- **Continuous enrichment** - keep CRM records updated with fresh Lusha data automatically
- **AI-powered outreach** - let TA4's AI agents call and qualify prospects using Lusha contact details
- **Reduced manual work** - eliminate data entry between prospecting and outreach so your team stays focused on conversion

## **How it works**

Lusha surfaces verified contact data - emails, direct dials, job titles, and company details. The Augmented 4's AI agents pick up that data and run automated qualification calls and personalized outreach sequences. All activity feeds back into your CRM, keeping your pipeline accurate without manual input.

<Note>
  To set up this integration, you need an active account with both Lusha and The Augmented 4. Contact your TA4 account team to enable the Lusha connection.
</Note>
