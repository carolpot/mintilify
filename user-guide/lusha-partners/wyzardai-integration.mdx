Wyzard.ai is a revenue intelligence platform that connects your CRM, marketing automation tools, and data sources — including Lusha — into one system. The integration eliminates the manual steps between identifying a prospect and having a complete, enriched record ready for action in your CRM.

Instead of exporting tables, running separate enrichment jobs, and re-importing results, the entire process happens automatically within your existing tech stack.

## **How it works**

1. **Connect your sources** — link your CRM, marketing automation tools, and Lusha through the Wyzard.ai platform.
2. **Define your triggers** — set the events that should kick off enrichment. For example: a contact form submission, three emails opens in a week, or two minutes spent on your pricing page.
3. **Enrich in real time** — when a trigger fires, Wyzard.ai queries Lusha immediately. Lusha returns verified emails, direct phone numbers, job titles, employee count, industry, and other firmographic data.
4. **Sync back automatically** — the enriched record lands in your CRM, complete and ready for segmentation, routing, or outreach — no manual steps required.

<Note>
  Real-time enrichment matters most when a prospect is showing buying intent. Wyzard.ai is built to act on those signals the moment they happen, so your team can engage while the prospect is still active.
</Note>

## **What Lusha returns**

When Wyzard.ai queries Lusha during an enrichment, you get:

- Verified work email addresses
- Direct phone numbers
- Accurate job titles and seniority
- Company size, industry classification, and revenue range
- Other firmographic data points

## **How to get started**

Setup is managed within your Wyzard.ai account. Connect Lusha as a data source, configure your trigger events, and map the enriched fields to your CRM. For setup guidance, contact the Wyzard.ai support team or refer to their documentation.

<Note>
  You'll need an active Lusha account with a valid API key. Your API key is available in **API Hub → Manage API Keys** (visible to Admins and Managers only, in Premium and Scale plans).
</Note>

You're all set to enrich and act on high-intent prospects the moment they show up in your pipeline.
