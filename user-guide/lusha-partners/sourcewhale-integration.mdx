SourceWhale is a multi-channel outreach and recruitment automation platform. The Lusha integration lets you pull verified contact data - emails and phone numbers - directly into SourceWhale, so you can find, enrich, and engage prospects without switching between tools.

All activity is automatically synced back to your connected CRM (such as Salesforce or HubSpot), keeping your records clean and your team focused on conversations, not admin.

## **What you can do with this integration**

- **Data enrichment** - use Lusha contact data (emails, phone numbers) to fuel SourceWhale outreach sequences
- **Multi-channel automation** - schedule sequences across email, LinkedIn, and phone calls from within SourceWhale, personalized at scale
- **CRM/ATS sync** - all outreach activity and enriched data logs back to your CRM automatically, keeping records complete
- **Unified workflow** - source, enrich, engage, and track from one platform, without constant tab-switching

## **How to connect Lusha to SourceWhale**

Setup is managed within your SourceWhale account. Follow SourceWhale's step-by-step integration guide to authenticate Lusha and start pulling contact data into your sequences:

<Warning>
  Lusha API Key is only available to Lusha Premium and Scale plans customers and is an additional add-on.
</Warning>

Navigate to your initials/image in the bottom left-hand corner > Select Integrations from the dropdown menu.

</Note>

## **How it works**

Once connected, SourceWhale pulls Lusha contact data - verified emails and direct dials - into your outreach sequences. You define your target table in Lusha, and SourceWhale handles the multi-channel engagement and CRM logging automatically. No manual exports, no copy-pasting between platforms.

You're all set to source, enrich, and engage your prospects in one seamless recruitment workflow.
