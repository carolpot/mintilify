Once you've composed your sequence emails, the next step is configuring when they'll be sent. You control both the timing of each individual email and the overall sending window for the sequence.

## Set the send time for each email

At the top of the email builder, use the **Send this email** box to choose when each step is sent. You have two options:

- **On activation** - the email sends as soon as the sequence goes live.
- **Custom timing** - send the email a set number of days after the previous step.

## Set up the sequence sending window

The sequence schedule controls which days and times emails are sent. Choose your sending days, time range, and time zone.

<img src="https://lusha.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/18922682/d097f8f89a3582b7de17125f/AD_4nXc_b52EkHuySeb_LL5UgZ3GKcc30vMzlS7xSZ7Ft4A00sB-OzJn86UMqRalWdh5BnRx0kPsY4gR3RGd2q8vorq4CWUSOQkv2E4HgfrJ4nDG8X15vKshX-qXJnTvUPWS5a1yRCEKg5eezZUaKaHjigu4V0s?expires=1780056000&signature=ebbd7d3ffe26172b2c5d617422afd46dd9b47ebce1705a22639fe0b2158b735e&req=0d1mx1r8pDVk2hj99dpg6rKf%2BPKjLVdEGPxqevnNEv3w4qtmS6DMO1bXjRU0%0Ac03fEdjgphYuRDkro4U2Qj76%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

<Note>
  Each sequence supports one time zone only. If your contacts are in multiple time zones, create separate sequences for each region.
</Note>

Email intervals (e.g., "every 3 days") only count days within your selected sending window. Days outside the window are skipped.

Once your schedule is set, click **Review and activate** to launch your sequence.
