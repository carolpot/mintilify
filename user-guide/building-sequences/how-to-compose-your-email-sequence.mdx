Sequences are outreach campaigns consisting of multiple sequential contact points and tasks that you can tailor to personally engage your target audiences on a large scale. Strategic planning and execution are key to optimizing these sequences for better engagement and achieving specific goals such as replies, clicks, or conversions.

Additionally, Lusha Engage supports both manual and automated email sending, allowing you to tailor your outreach strategy to your specific goals and audience preferences.

Sequences enable your team to communicate effectively with customers and complete the appropriate tasks at the optimal times, accelerating the progress of prospects through your sales pipeline and converting them into paying customers as efficiently as possible. Incorporating best practices such as crafting compelling content, targeting the right audience, and structuring sequences effectively can further enhance communication and conversion rates.

Furthermore, you can choose between manual and automated email sending options to align with your campaign objectives and audience needs.

## Best Practices for Improving Email Outreach

To enhance the performance of your email sequences, consider the following best practices:

1. **Craft Compelling Content**

   - Ensure your message is clear, relevant, and includes a strong call to action.
   - Personalize the content to make it feel genuine and tailored to the recipient.
2. **Target the Right Audience**

   - Verify that your emails are reaching the appropriate audience for your goals.
3. **Structure Your Sequences Effectively**

   - Plan sequences with multiple touchpoints, such as 3–5 follow-ups over 2–3 weeks.
   - Mix communication channels, like email and LinkedIn, to increase visibility.
4. **Test and Iterate**

   - Experiment with subject lines, tones, and calls-to-action to identify what performs best.

## Reply in thread

When building a sequence, you can choose to send follow-up emails as a reply to the previous message or as a separate email. To ensure your 2nd, 3rd, or subsequent emails are sent within the same conversation, simply check the **"Send as reply to previous thread"** box; Engage also detects replies left in your inbox for a short period (10–15 minutes) and automatically stops the sequence for that contact, ensuring no redundant follow-ups are sent.

## Reorder Steps in Your Sequence

You can now drag and drop steps to reorder them directly in the sequence editor. This makes it easy to adjust your outreach flow without deleting and rebuilding steps.

### To reorder a step:

1. Open the sequence in the editor.
2. Hover over the step you want to move — a drag handle will appear.
3. Click and drag the step to the new position in the sequence.
4. Release to drop it in place.

## FAQs

**Why do I have a high open rate but a low reply rate?**

This typically indicates that while your subject lines are effective, the message content or targeting may not be compelling enough. Focus on personalization, clarity, and a strong call to action to improve reply rates.

**How can I improve traction with my outreach emails?**

Use tools like Lusha's AI Assistant and templates, personalize your messages, and plan structured sequences with multiple touchpoints.

**How does Engage detect replies and stop sequences?**

Engage detects replies left in your inbox for a short period (10–15 minutes) and automatically stops the sequence for that contact.
