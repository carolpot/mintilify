Beyond Salesforce and HubSpot, Lusha natively integrates with several other CRM and sales-engagement platforms. Setup follows a similar pattern across all of them: connect the account, map fields, and choose whether existing records can be overridden.

## Supported platforms

| Platform | Typical export limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pipedrive | 25 contacts per export | Maps to Contacts and Organizations |
| Outreach | 25 contacts per export | Save-to-sequence available |
| Salesloft | 25 contacts per export | Save-to-cadence available |
| Bullhorn | 25 contacts per export | Maps to Leads, Candidates, Contacts, and Companies |
| Zoho CRM | 50 contacts per export | — |
| Microsoft Dynamics | 25 contacts per export | — |

<Note>
  Export limits are set by Lusha and may change — check the export dialog in your account for the current limit on each platform.
</Note>

## Connecting an integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations settings">
    In Lusha, go to **Settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your platform">
    Choose the CRM or sales-engagement tool you want to connect, and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Sign in with an admin account on the target platform to authorize the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure field mapping">
    Map Lusha fields to the corresponding fields on the other platform for each supported record type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set export preferences">
    Optionally enable **Allow Override** to update existing records, and set predefined or conditional field values.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Admins can typically restrict which record types (for example, new accounts or organizations) team members are allowed to create on export, limiting exports to updates on existing records only.
</Info>

## Pipedrive

Pushes enriched contact and organization data into Pipedrive. Default mappings send full name, email, and phone to Contacts, and company name and address to Organizations.

## Outreach and Salesloft

Both integrations push enriched prospect and account data into your sequences or cadences. Outreach maps to Prospects and Accounts; Salesloft maps to People and Accounts. Both support adding contacts to a sequence or cadence directly from an export.

## Bullhorn

Built for recruiting workflows, Bullhorn's integration maps enriched data to Leads, Candidates, Contacts, and Companies, with identifier-based duplicate checking before saving a record.

## Zapier, Clay, and other connectors

Lusha also connects with workflow tools like Zapier, Clay, and Make for custom automations, along with a handful of Lusha partner integrations (for example, Chili Piper, monday.com, and SourceWhale). <!-- VERIFY: could not confirm full current partner list or setup details on live site -->
