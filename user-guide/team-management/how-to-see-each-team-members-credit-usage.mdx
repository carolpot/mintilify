---
title: "How to see each team member's credit usage"
description: "How Admins and Managers can view per-user credit consumption, export usage reports, and set up automated reporting."
---

As an Admin or Manager, you can see exactly how many credits each team member has used - broken down by user, feature, and credit type. This helps you track spending, spot unusually high usage, and decide whether to adjust credit limits.

<Note>
New to credits? Start with [How credits work in Lusha](/user-guide/getting-started/how-credits-work-in-lusha) for the basics - plan allowances, rollover, and reset dates.
</Note>

<Note>
Viewing team credit usage requires an **Admin** or **Manager** role. Available on Pro, Premium, and Scale plans.
</Note>

## Option 1: view usage in Analytics

The Analytics dashboard gives you a real-time snapshot of your team's credit usage for the current billing cycle.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Analytics">
    Click your name in the left navigation and select **Analytics**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your team's totals">
    The top of the page shows your team's total credits, credits used, and credits remaining.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the per-user breakdown">
    Scroll down to the **Usage by team member** table to see a per-user breakdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by date range">
    Use the **date range selector** to filter by a specific period within the current cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The Analytics dashboard shows data for the current billing cycle only. To view usage across past billing periods, use Option 2 to export a report.
</Note>

## Option 2: export a usage report

For a full breakdown - by user, group, and credit type - you can export a CSV report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Analytics">
    Click your name in the left navigation and select **Analytics**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the usage report">
    Click **Usage report**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the CSV">
    The report downloads as a CSV file showing each team member's credit consumption broken down by feature (Platform, Extension, API, Workspace).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Option 3: set up automated usage reports

Schedule a recurring usage report to be sent to your inbox - useful for monthly billing reviews or team check-ins.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Report settings">
    Go to **Account → Report settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on usage reports">
    Toggle on **Usage reports activation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose frequency and recipients">
    Choose your frequency (**Weekly** or **Monthly**) and add up to 5 recipient email addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the schedule">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Your Lusha account settings - a complete guide](/user-guide/user-setup/your-lusha-account-settings-a-complete-guide#report-settings) for the full Report settings reference.

## How to set credit limits per user

If you want to cap how much each person can spend, you can set per-user credit limits from **Account → Credit limits** (Scale plan only).

See [How to limit credits per user or group](/user-guide/manage-your-team-capabilities/how-to-limit-credits-per-user-or-group) for step-by-step instructions.

Now you have full visibility into how credits are being used across your team.
