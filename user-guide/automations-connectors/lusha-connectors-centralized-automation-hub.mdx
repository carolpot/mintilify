Lusha Connectors allow you to bridge the gap between Lusha's database and your daily tech stack. By integrating with leading automation platforms, you can eliminate manual data entry, trigger real-time enrichment, and ensure your sales team always has the most accurate contact information at their fingertips.

You can browse all available connectors, use cases, and ready-to-use templates in the **[Connectors](https://dashboard.lusha.com/api/connectors)** tab inside **API Hub** (left sidebar).

## How to Get Started

Connecting Lusha to any of these tools follows the same three-step process:

1. **Copy your API Key:** Open **API Hub** from the left sidebar and click **Copy API key** in the top right (Admin or Manager role required).
2. **Authenticate:** In your chosen automation tool, search for "Lusha Connect" and paste your API key when prompted.
3. **Map your Fields:** Choose your trigger (e.g., a new lead) and map the necessary input fields (Email or LinkedIn URL) to the Lusha action.

For platform-specific steps, triggers, actions, and ready-to-use plays, follow the setup guide for your connector in the table above.
