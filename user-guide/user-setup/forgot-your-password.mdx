If you've forgotten your Lusha password, you can reset it directly from the login page in a few steps.

## How to reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the login page">
    Go to the [Lusha login page](https://dashboard.lusha.co/login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a password reset">
    Click **Forgot Password** and enter your email address.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/yu27vhse/55462063/68867f3c93bbaf294e863c26146b/image.png?expires=1780056000&signature=0d5b7f3d216017874ff18f44b834834b77ba52018def174956b1b382079c532f&req=1dBrw1r6qjRk2hj99dpg6pAkkOL9qfV3n2fL%2FmrIQD4lSamS%2BrRwc7zFnqZl%0AH9H9GY2e3pdeMahuIB32Xtfu%0A" alt="Lusha login page with the Forgot Password link" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Check your inbox for a **Reset Password** email from Lusha. If you don't see it, check your spam or junk folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new password">
    Open the email and click the reset link to create a new password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your new password">
    Return to the [login page](https://dashboard.lusha.co/login) and sign in with your new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why is the sign-in button greyed out?

This usually means the email field isn't filled in correctly. Make sure you've entered a complete email address in the format *[yourname@example.com](mailto:yourname@example.com)* before clicking Sign in.

## Logging in via LinkedIn

If you use LinkedIn to log in and the sign-in button is greyed out, enter your full email address in the username field - this is required even when using LinkedIn as your login method.

## Still having trouble?

- Try clearing your browser cache and attempting to log in again.
- Check that your email provider or organisation's firewall isn't blocking emails from Lusha.

If the issue persists, contact Lusha Support using the chat icon in your dashboard.
