Your account settings are where you manage your profile, billing, team permissions, integrations, and usage reports. The options available to you depend on your role - User, Manager, or Admin - and your organisation's plan.

Access your settings by clicking your name at the bottom-left of the sidebar.

<Note>
Your Account Settings page has 8 tabs: **My profile**, **Payments & Subscription**, **Account settings**, **Report settings**, **Tags management**, **Intent**, **Privacy**, and **Exports**. The tabs visible to you depend on your role.
</Note>

## My profile

The **My Profile** tab shows your full name and email address.

To update your name:

1. Click on the name field.
2. Make your changes.
3. Click **Save changes**.

To change your email address, contact Lusha Support via the chat icon in your dashboard. If your new email is from the same company, the support team can help migrate your account. If it's from a different company, you'll need to create a new account under the new domain.

## Payments and Subscription

(Available for Admins and Managers only)

The **Payments & Subscription** tab lets you view your invoice history, update your payment method, and check your current plan, credit balance, and renewal date.

- To update billing details, see [Update your billing information](/user-guide/billing-and-payments/how-to-update-your-billing-information).
- To cancel your plan, see [How to cancel your Lusha subscription](/user-guide/billing-and-payments/how-to-cancel-your-lusha-subscription).

<Note>
Enterprise users may not have access to all billing options. Contact your Account Manager for assistance with plan changes.
</Note>

## Account settings

(Available for Admins and Managers only)

## Permission settings

Set what your Users are allowed to do:

- **Invite members** - allow Users to invite new members to the account.
- **Bulk show** - allow Users to reveal contact details in bulk. Limits by plan: Free: 25, Pro: 50, Premium: 150, Scale: 5,000 contacts at a time.
- **CSV export** - allow Users to export contacts to a CSV file. (Scale plan only.)
- **CSV enrichment** - allow Users to enrich CSV files with Lusha data. (Scale plan only.)

## Credit limits

(Scale plan only)

Set monthly or annual credit limits per user or group. The limit resets on the same day each cycle that your plan started. See [Limit credits per user or group](/user-guide/manage-your-team-capabilities/how-to-limit-credits-per-user-or-group) for setup steps.

## Inactivity timeout

(Scale plan only)

Automatically log out team members after a chosen period of inactivity. Set the duration in minutes, hours, or days to add a security layer and reduce the risk of unauthorised access.

## SSO - single sign-on

Configure SSO so all team members sign in using your company identity provider.

- See [Set up SSO (Okta/Azure)](/user-guide/security/how-to-set-up-sso-single-sign-on)
- See [Custom SAML single sign-on](/user-guide/security/custom-saml-single-sign-on)

## Report settings

(Available for Admins only)

The **Report settings** tab lets you schedule automated usage reports and send them to your team by email - no manual dashboard visits needed.

1. Toggle on **Usage reports activation**.
2. Choose your frequency: **Weekly** (Mon–Fri) or **Monthly** (calendar month).
3. Add up to 5 recipient email addresses.
4. Click **Save**.

## Tags management

(Available for Admins only)

The **Tags management** tab lets you view, create, edit, or delete contact tags for your entire team. Each tag shows a usage count - the number of contacts it's currently applied to.

<Note>
Renaming a tag updates it everywhere it's applied. Deleting a tag removes it from all contacts account-wide and cannot be undone. See [Contact tagging](/user-guide/getting-started-with-prospecting/contact-tagging) for how to apply tags to contacts.
</Note>

## Intent

(Available for Admins only)

The **Intent** tab is where you choose which topics Lusha monitors for your account. Your selections power intent signals in Workspace, the buying intent widget on your Dashboard, intent-based Search filters, and AI recommendations.

1. Use the keyword or category search to find topics.
2. Click a topic to add it to your tracked table.
3. To remove a topic, click the **X** next to it.
4. Changes save automatically.

<Note>
The number of topics you can select depends on your plan. Topics can be updated once a month. See [How to Configure Intent Topic Tracking](/user-guide/intent/how-to-use-lushas-intent-solution) for a full walkthrough.
</Note>

## Privacy

(Available for Admins and Managers only)

The **Privacy** tab lets you choose to hide contacts from specific countries in the Prospecting Platform, or exclude contacts registered on the Do Not Call (DNC) table in the US or UK.

## Exports

The **Exports** tab has two sections:

## Technology columns

Select which technology categories to include as columns in your CSV exports. If a company in your export uses a technology you've saved here, it will appear as a dedicated column in the downloaded file.

## Export history

View and re-download past CSV and CRM export files. Admins can access the full account export history; Users can access their own exports only.

<Note>
The **All Contacts** table does not appear in export history.
</Note>

You're all set - your account settings give you full control over how your team uses Lusha.
