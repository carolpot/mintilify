---
title: "How to change your password"
description: "How to update your Lusha account password from your dashboard settings."
---

You can update your Lusha password at any time from your account settings. Lusha sends a reset link to your registered email address - you don't set the new password directly in the dashboard.

## How to change your password

1. Log in to your [Lusha dashboard](https://dashboard.lusha.com/dashboard).
2. Click your name in the left navigation and select **Account & Settings**.
3. Go to the **[My Profile](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account/myProfile)** tab.
4. Click **Send me reset instructions**.
5. Check your inbox for a password reset email from Lusha.
6. Open the email and click **Choose a new password**.

7. Enter and confirm your new password.

<Note>

</Note>
