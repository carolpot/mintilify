title: "How to sign up to Lusha?"
description: "How to create a free Lusha account using a business email address and verify with a 6-digit code."
---

To create a free Lusha account, you need a business email address — for example, **[john@yourcompany.com](mailto:john@yourcompany.com)**. Lusha does not accept public email domains (Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, etc.) or generic prefixes like info@, sales@, hr@, or support@.

## How to sign up

1. Go to the [Lusha signup page](https://auth.lusha.com/signup) and enter your work email address, first name, last name, job title, and phone number.

<img src="https://lusha-eu.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/13259676/f22bc41eca61439334134e21/Screen%2BShot%2B2022-10-25%2Bat%2B16_19_57.png?expires=1780056000&signature=1209e025b4bee950e4ea51bd8c8968d2f4d3ee8b8aed45182a1ef9837fd58255&req=0dZtwFH8qzFk2hj99dpg6m04zSXbrM35ryhEt1UAD8Q%2BAh5totmI8p78uYPl%0A9SmpLpQsM37JP6QzMH9hedWi%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

1. Click **Get started**. A verification email with a 6-digit code will be sent to your inbox.
2. Enter the code. You'll be redirected to your [Lusha Dashboard](https://dashboard.lusha.com/dashboard) and you're ready to go.

<Warning>
Didn't receive your 6-digit code? Check your spam folder or try signing up again. If you still don't receive it, contact support via live chat from the [Lusha website](http://www.lusha.com/), your [Lusha Dashboard](http://dashboard.lusha.com/dashboard), or at [support@lusha.com](mailto:support@lusha.com).
</Warning>

<Note>
If your email address is being flagged as invalid or suspicious, see [Why does my email address appear as "invalid" or suspicious?](/user-guide/user-setup/why-does-my-email-address-appear-as-invalid-or-suspicious)
</Note>

You're all set — welcome to Lusha!
