The Lusha Slack app brings contact and company data directly into Slack. You can run quick lookups with slash commands or have a conversation with the Lusha Agent to find decision-makers, enrich contacts, and push data to your CRM — all without switching tabs.

## What the Lusha Slack App Includes

The app has two capabilities in a single install:

- **Lusha Agent** — a conversational agent you can @mention in any channel or DM. Ask it to find contacts, summarize account activity, or identify decision-makers in plain English.
- **Slash Commands** — `/lusha` for contact lookups and `/lusha_company` for company snapshots.

## Before You Start

- An active Lusha account (Free plan or above)
- Workspace Admin access in Slack (for the initial install)
- The install is free — it works with your existing Lusha credit balance

## How to Install Lusha for Slack

1. **Click Add to Slack** from the Lusha for Slack page. This opens the standard Slack OAuth authorization flow.
2. **Authorize the app** in your Slack workspace. One admin install covers the entire workspace — no per-user setup required.
3. **Sign in with your existing Lusha credentials.** Your credit balance and watch-list sync automatically.

You're set up when you see the Lusha app appear in your Slack Apps sidebar. The whole process takes under 2 minutes.

## Using Slash Commands

### /lusha — Contact Lookup

Type `/lusha [name] at [company]` in any channel or DM. For example: `/lusha Jane Smith at Stripe`.

Lusha returns a contact card with name, title, company, and LinkedIn URL. The results are ephemeral — only you can see them.

To reveal contact details, click **Reveal email & phone** or choose to reveal email or phone individually. Each reveal costs 1 credit. Search is always free.

<Note>
Results are visible only to you. No contact data is stored by the Slack app layer.
</Note>

### /lusha_company — Company Snapshot

Type `/lusha_company [company name]` to pull a company overview: headcount, industry, tech stack, and funding stage.

Company lookups are free — no credits are consumed.

## Using the Lusha Agent

The Lusha Agent responds to natural-language queries. Type `@Lusha` in any channel or DM to start.

**Things you can ask the agent:**

- Find the VP of Sales at a specific company
- Summarize recent signals for a target account
- Identify decision-makers at a company
- Run a bulk contact or company search

The agent keeps thread context, so you can ask follow-up questions within the same conversation.

<Note>
The Lusha Agent can push enriched contacts directly to Salesforce or HubSpot if your CRM integration is already configured in your Lusha account.
</Note>

## AI Model Disclosure

The Lusha Agent uses large language models to interpret your queries and structure results. Here's what each provider receives:

| Provider | What is sent |
|---|---|
| OpenAI (GPT-5.4 mini) | Your query text and prior thread messages in the same conversation. No contact PII or credit data. |
| Lusha API | Structured search parameters from your query. Contact data is returned directly to you — never to the LLM. |

<Note>
The AI models are not trained on your workspace data and do not make automated consequential decisions. Full sub-processor list: [lusha.com/sub-processors](https://lusha.com/sub-processors)
</Note>

## Privacy & Security

- **No PII stored** — Contact data passes through in memory only. Nothing is logged or persisted in the Slack app layer.
- **Minimal OAuth scopes** — Only `chat:write`, `commands`, and `im:history`. No access to workspace message history.
- **Encrypted API tokens** — Your Lusha API token is encrypted at rest and revocable from App Home → Settings at any time.
- **Regional compliance** — US contact data restrictions are enforced at the API level.
