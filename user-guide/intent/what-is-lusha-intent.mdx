Lusha Intent uses behavioral signals to identify companies that are actively researching topics relevant to your product. Instead of reaching out based on static firmographic data alone, Intent data helps you time your outreach to accounts that are already in a buying mindset.

## What Intent data shows you

The Intent filter consolidates all intent signals into a single view, scored and ranked so the most relevant accounts appear first. Each company is given a level of intent -**Very Hot**, **Hot**, or **Warm** - based on how many topics they're engaging with and at what intensity.

Intent scores are based on Bombora's Company Surge® data, which tracks research activity across a co-operative network of over 5,000 B2B websites. Lusha measures weekly changes in content consumption against each company's 12-week baseline, flagging accounts that are significantly increasing their research on topics relevant to your product.

## How the scoring works

Companies are ranked using a topic-synergy scoring model that combines the number of related topics a company is researching with the signal strength of each topic. Key elements include the topic count (how many of your selected topics the company is engaging with), the level of intent (Very Hot, Hot, Warm), and the topic count trend, which shows whether intent is rising or falling week-over-week.

This approach reduces false signals and surfaces accounts that are showing consistent, meaningful research activity rather than one-off spikes.

## What's changed recently

**LinkedIn Activity Intent - the first Social Signal:** A related but separate capability, now live in the API and MCP - it surfaces companies whose employees are posting on LinkedIn about topics mapped to the same Bombora intent categories used here, so you can catch buying signals as they're posted rather than waiting for the weekly Company Surge refresh. See the Signals API guide (Social Signals section). Workspace availability is rolling out next.

**Intent as a Workspace Table enrichment:** Intent is no longer just a Search filter - you can now add it as a signal enrichment directly on Workspace Tables (Signals panel → Intent category), surfacing signal date, previous week score, this week's score, and week-over-week trend as table columns. See Workspace Signals: Tracking Buying Intent and Contact Changes for setup details.

**Reset intent topics:** Admins can now reset intent topics directly from **Settings → Intent**, without contacting support. Resetting is available every 30 days from the date the last topic was added.

**Unified Intent experience:** The Intent tab and Intent filter have been merged into a single, streamlined system.

**Enhanced data quality:** The topic-synergy model reduces false signals and focuses on accounts showing meaningful research patterns, not noise.

## About Lusha's Intent data

Lusha ingests [Bombora's Company Surge®](https://bombora.com/) data, which is ethically sourced through opt-in consent from both publishers and users, and is CCPA and GDPR compliant. Because Bombora's intent data refreshes weekly, Lusha customers always have current, actionable buying signals to act on.

## FAQs

**How many results can I see?**\
Self-service plans (Professional/Pro) can see up to 25 Intent results. Scale plans have full access and see results based on their selected filters and topics.

**Who can use Intent?**\
All Lusha users can access the Intent filter. Full export capabilities (up to 10K companies) are exclusive to Scale users.

**What is the topic count trend?**\
It tracks weekly changes in the number of topic hits for each company, so you can monitor whether their buying intent is increasing, flat, or declining.

**Can I reset my intent topics?**\
Yes - Admins can reset topics from **Settings → Intent** once every 30 days from the date the last topic was added.
