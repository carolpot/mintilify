---
title: "Coaching your team with Lusha Conversations"
description: "Use call recordings and conversation metrics to coach your sales team with data-driven feedback."
---

The **Coaching** tab in Lusha Conversations surfaces call-level data to help managers identify skill gaps, reinforce winning behaviors, and give specific, evidence-based feedback. Instead of sitting in on calls, you can review key moments from any recorded meeting and use the data in your 1:1s.

## How to access the Coaching tab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Conversations">
    Go to **Engagement → Conversations** (/ci/meetings) in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a recorded meeting">
    Click on any recorded meeting to open its Meeting Analysis page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Coaching tab">
    Click the **Coaching** tab in the top-right corner of the analysis page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Coaching tab shows

The Coaching tab surfaces deeper verbal communication metrics and conversation patterns for individual meetings, including:

- Talk time ratio - how much the rep spoke vs. the prospect
- Longest monologue duration
- Questions asked per call
- Filler word frequency
- Key topic coverage based on your tracked topics

## How to use Coaching data in team reviews

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a rep's recorded meeting">
    Open a recorded meeting for a rep on your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Coaching tab">
    Click the **Coaching** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the metrics">
    Review the metrics shown. Look for patterns - high talk ratio, low question count, or missed topic coverage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clip a moment to share">
    Use the **Snippets** feature to clip a specific moment to share with the rep. See "Using the Snippets Library" in the using-the-platform section for how to create and share snippets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discuss it in your next 1:1">
    In your next 1:1, open the coaching data together and discuss specific moments from the recording.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The **Insights** tab shows aggregate data across all meetings - useful for spotting team-wide trends. The **Coaching** tab is for individual meeting analysis. See "Lusha Conversations Insights" in the using-the-platform section for the team-level view.
</Note>

Use your call data to build a stronger, more consistent sales team.
