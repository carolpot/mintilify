---
title: "Using the Snippets Library"
description: "How to save, find, and share short clips from recorded meetings using the Lusha Conversations Snippets Library."
---

The Snippets Library is a shared team resource inside Lusha Conversations where you can save, browse, and replay short clips from recorded meetings.

Use it to capture moments worth keeping - a strong objection handle, a compelling discovery question, a powerful demo moment, or a prospect quote. Snippets give your whole team a searchable library of real examples to learn from.

## How to access the Snippets Library

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Conversations">
    Go to **Engagement → Conversations** in the left navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Snippets library tab">
    Click the **Snippets library** tab at the top of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do in the Snippets Library

- Browse all snippets saved across your team
- Filter by **Category** or **Sub-category**
- Search by snippet name using the search bar
- See who created each snippet and when
- Sort by creation date or number of views

## How to create a snippet

Snippets are created from inside a Meeting Analysis page. You need at least one completed recorded meeting to get started.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a recorded meeting">
    Open a recorded meeting from your **Conversations** table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the moment">
    In the transcript or playback, navigate to the moment you want to save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the clip">
    Select the clip you want to capture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and categorize the snippet">
    Give the snippet a name, and assign a **Category** and **Sub-category**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the snippet">
    Save the snippet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The snippet is immediately added to the shared Snippets Library and is visible to all members of your Lusha account.

<Note>
Snippets are visible to everyone on your account. There is currently no option to create private snippets.
</Note>

Now your team has a growing library of real-call examples to learn from.
