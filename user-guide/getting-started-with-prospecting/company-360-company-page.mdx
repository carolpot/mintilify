title: "Company 360 - Company page"
description: "How to use Lusha's Company 360 page to research target accounts, view employees, funding, technologies, and intent signals."
---

The Company 360 page gives you a full picture of any target company - its firmographic details, employee map, funding history, technologies, and intent signals - all without leaving your Lusha dashboard. Use it to research accounts before reaching out or to find the right contacts within a specific organization.

## How to open a company page

Click the **[Search](https://dashboard.lusha.com/prospecting/companies)** tab in the left menu, switch to the **Companies** view, and type a company name in the filter panel or upper search bar. Click on any company result to open its dedicated page.

## What's on the company page

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/yu27vhse/39224775/e2ae30ae5b1f3d2a52c225601240/Screenshot+2025-02-24+at+10_19_20.png?expires=1780056000&signature=0190a1c12cc28d1de07829481e1ccdbf6ba6f37f6411906594bb1f6411dffe05&req=09xtx1z9qzJk2hj99dpg6nGMK6gHXaVRpnH5Pw7y1MK1rcOUc7fbV03ijcdZ%0AaUQB0QjEYZmaSG%2B1pPGt3jhE%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

**Company overview:** The left panel shows HQ location, industry, company size, founding year, SIC/NAICS codes, and specialties, along with a description of the company's product and market.

**Employee statistics:** The Employees Statistics section shows a breakdown of decision-makers by seniority and department. Click any seniority level to jump directly to matching contacts in Search. Use the department dropdown under **Potential Decision Makers** to narrow further.

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/yu27vhse/39225014/0b69910f26982098ac3aa8b9a40d/Screenshot%2B2025-02-24%2Bat%2B10_21_00.png?expires=1780056000&signature=ffa80e27b13057fa900272ef9dda8c07312f7673e9fee064c5831f7c666d69b8&req=09xtx136rTNk2hj99dpg6g1uMEj7006A83CjvF7DNRuqP1YImte9upPPJfU1%0ADSy1WUIGP4fer9qYXvIgcRFv%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

To save contacts directly from the company page, click the **Save to** button and choose a table or CRM.

**Technologies:** The top-right panel shows the technologies the company uses, based on Lusha's proprietary technographic model.

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/yu27vhse/39225084/ac61961882e0cae2e1ffb4284eac/Screenshot+2025-02-24+at+10_22_04.png?expires=1780056000&signature=8ee1cc080b42e45c1257af860d06cd7e4c749d9f0a35a4cb999ee6c63da6419e&req=09xtx136pDNk2hj99dpg6nvNtLD%2FZlZE%2FDcZDW4u901Ps9rC%2FK3q9sDRlLu8%0AppKVXD9TelNRdKtt8DtsX7Yz%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

**Intent topics:** If you've set up intent topics in your account settings, the company page will show whether this company is actively researching relevant subjects. Learn more about [how to use Intent](/user-guide/intent/how-to-use-lushas-intent-solution).

**Funding:** The bottom of the page shows total funding amount, number of rounds, and a year-by-year breakdown. Click **Funding rounds** to see each round's details.

**Similar companies:** The bottom-right panel surfaces companies that closely match the one you're viewing. Click any suggestion to open its full company page.

## Filtering employees on the company page

To view all employees, click the **View employees** button in the upper-right area of the company page. Use the **Seniority** filter to narrow the employee table to the level you're targeting.
