---
title: "Contact tagging"
description: "How to add, manage, and filter contact tags in Lusha Search, the extension, and contact tables."
---

Tags let you categorise and organise your contacts in Lusha. You can tag contacts from Lusha Search, the extension, or your contact tables, and filter by tag to find the right prospects quickly.

<Note>
Admins can manage all tags in the account. Users can only manage their own tags, but can view and use tags created by others.
</Note>

<Note>
Tagging is available only when using the Lusha Prospecting platform.
</Note>

---

## Adding tags from a Contact Table

You can tag contacts directly from a Contact Table, but only after the contacts have been saved to a table.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a contact table">
    Go to **Contact Tables** and open a table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select contacts">
    Select the contacts you want to tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Tag">
    Click **Tag** in the action bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or create tags and apply">
    Choose or create tags, then click **Apply**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Adding tags in Lusha Search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reveal the contacts">
    Go to **Prospect Search** and reveal the contacts you want to tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select contacts">
    Select the contacts (up to 150 at once), then click **Tags** in the action bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or create a tag">
    Choose an existing tag or type a new name and click **Create tag**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the tag">
    Click **Apply**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Adding tags from the Lusha extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reveal a contact">
    Open the extension and reveal a contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + Add tags">
    Click **+ Add tags**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or create a tag and apply">
    Choose an existing tag or create a new one, then click **Apply**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a tag, click on the tag to open the tag box, deselect it, and click **Apply**.

---

## Filtering contacts by tag

Go to your [All Contacts table](https://dashboard.lusha.com/tables/contacts/all-contact) or any contact table, click the **Tags** filter, and select the tags you want. The table updates automatically.

---

## Exporting tags to your CRM

To sync tags to your CRM, go to your integration's **Field Mapping** settings and map the **Contact Tags (String)** field in Lusha to a corresponding string field in your CRM. Tags will then export automatically with the contact's data.

---

## Managing tags

Go to the [Tag Management](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account/tagsManager) tab in your account settings to create, edit, or delete tags for your entire account.

---

You're all set - tags help you stay organised and find the right contacts faster.
