---
title: "Company Bulk Search"
description: "How to search for up to 10,000 companies at once in Lusha using bulk company search, by typing names or uploading a CSV."
---

Company Bulk Search lets you search for multiple companies at once by entering names or URLs directly, or by uploading a CSV file. It's a fast way to prospect multiple target accounts in a single search instead of looking them up one at a time.

<Note>
The number of companies you can upload depends on your plan. Scale plan users (Workspace & Prospecting) can upload up to **10,000 companies** per bulk search. All other plans can upload up to **100 companies**.
</Note>

## How to run a bulk company search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Search tab">
    Click the **[Search](https://dashboard.lusha.com/prospecting/companies)** tab in the left dashboard menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Companies">
    Switch to the **Companies** tab, or click **Companies** on the search bar.

    <Frame>
    <img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/18222903/cb3cc441b2ce5d50d8faec13/Screenshot+2024-08-11+at+13_46_57.png?expires=1780056000&signature=d156955d53f57cd29896b2d4a5713be4c69841283def58335b9722efa4b5f1ac&req=0d1tx1rzrDRk2hj99dpg6ri%2BWpNlyjWDV09%2BB%2Fwf4P6DTPhnw60Dp%2FHr8yxG%0Aj1WkkFVZif%2FE%2Fxoa6P%2BC2Ae6%0A" alt="Companies tab in the Search dashboard" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Company name filter">
    Click on the **Company name** filter to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open bulk search">
    Click **Bulk search companies** to open the dedicated search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter or upload company names">
    Either type company names or URLs, or upload a CSV file containing one column of company names or URLs.

    <Note>
    If uploading a CSV, make sure it contains only one column. If your first row is a header, check the box to indicate this before uploading.
    </Note>

    <Frame>
    <img src="https://lusha-eu.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/13259740/b5acbe21b94471b8907519a9/Screen%2BShot%2B2022-11-04%2Bat%2B14_30_55.png?expires=1780056000&signature=848d7a765fcd12c7c0e3bb13629a154938531364efc12966cf6d2234c0ddd27d&req=0dZtwFH9qDdk2hj99dpg6vG7AfIcqSHZJoiqVny52Mp83AlA%2Fapt8gZS1voI%0AauIPTSBXM54Uuqt47OGsl2XA%0A" alt="Bulk search companies box for entering names or uploading a CSV" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your results">
    Click **View Results** to see your matched companies with the most up-to-date information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From the results, you can reveal contacts, save companies to tables, export to CSV, or push data directly to your CRM.
