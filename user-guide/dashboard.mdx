When you log in to Lusha, you land on the dashboard — your home base for prospecting, managing tables, and accessing account settings.

## Left navigation

The left-hand navigation panel gives you access to Lusha's core areas:

- **Home** — a dashboard overview, including quick search and AI-generated prospect recommendations.
- **Prospecting** — build prospect lists using search and filters.
- **Tables** — your workspace tables, including recents, AI recommendations, and website visitor tracking.
- **Tools** — API access, MCP integrations, engagement (Engage) features, and CSV enrichment.

## Account menu

Click your initials or profile icon in the bottom-left corner to open the account menu. From here you can reach:

- Your profile and login details
- Billing and subscription information
- Permission and account settings
- Team management
- Integrations

## Home screen

The home screen surfaces:

- A quick search bar to jump straight into finding contacts or companies
- Recent activity, so you can pick up where you left off
- AI-recommended leads based on your prospecting history

<Tip>
  Lusha refreshes AI-recommended leads daily. If you've saved a table, you can also generate similar prospects on demand using the "Add similar contacts" option inside that table.
</Tip>

## Getting oriented

If you're new to Lusha, a common first path through the product looks like this:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore the dashboard">
    Get familiar with the left navigation, home screen, and account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your first table">
    Head to Workspace or Prospecting and build a table using AI chat or filters — see [Search & lists](/user-guide/search-and-lists).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    Add the [Lusha browser extension](/user-guide/chrome-extension) so you can reveal contact data anywhere you work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your CRM">
    Link [Salesforce](/user-guide/salesforce) or [HubSpot](/user-guide/hubspot) so enriched data flows straight into your existing records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor your credits">
    Keep an eye on your credit balance under **Payments & Subscription** — see [Billing & plans](/user-guide/billing-and-plans).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Related settings

Most account-wide configuration — profile details, payments, permissions, credit limits, SSO, report scheduling, tags, and privacy controls — lives under **Settings → Account & settings**. See [Team management](/user-guide/team-management) and [Privacy & compliance](/user-guide/privacy-and-compliance) for details on the settings most relevant to admins.
