---

You can add contacts to a Lusha Engage sequence directly from the Lusha extension on LinkedIn. You can add a single contact or up to 25 contacts at a time.

## How to add contacts from the extension

Open the Lusha extension on a LinkedIn profile or search results page.

1. Reveal the contact's details.
2. Click **Sequence** below the contact's name.
3. Choose an existing sequence or create a new one.
4. Click **Add to sequence**.
