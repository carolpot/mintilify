<Note>
**Tables is the new name for tables.** If you use the Lusha extension or prospecting platform, nothing has changed - your contacts are now saved to **Tables** instead of **tables**. All features work exactly the same way. [Learn more.](/user-guide/introduction-and-getting-started/moving-from-classic-tables-to-workspaces)
</Note>

The one-time email feature lets you send a single, personalised email to any contact directly from your Tables - without setting up a sequence. It's useful for initial outreach, ad-hoc follow-ups, or any situation where automation isn't needed.

<Note>
You'll need a connected mailbox before you can send. See [Connect your email account](/user-guide/getting-started-with-engage/what-is-lusha-engage) if you haven't done this yet.
</Note>

<Note>
Sent emails will appear in your Gmail or Outlook **Sent** folder. Replies from prospects will arrive in your personal inbox - not inside the Lusha platform.
</Note>

## How one-time emails affect your sending limits

Lusha Engage applies daily and hourly sending limits per mailbox to help maintain deliverability for automated sequences. One-time emails are sent immediately and are not queued, but they **do count toward these limits**. This means sending manual emails can reduce the number of sequence emails allowed to go out in the same time window.

For example, if your hourly limit is 6 emails and you send 2 manual emails at 10:15 AM, only 4 of your 6 scheduled sequence emails can go out before 11:00 AM. The remaining 2 will be rescheduled to the next available hour.

<Note>
Be mindful of manual sends to avoid delaying your sequences or risking deliverability issues from high email volume.
</Note>
