Engage is Lusha's built-in sales engagement module. Once you've found and revealed your prospects, you can send them personalized email sequences directly from the platform - without switching to a separate tool. Engage connects to your existing Gmail or Outlook mailbox and handles outreach from there.

It's designed for sales reps who want to move quickly from prospecting to outreach in one place, with less manual setup.

## What you can do with Engage

- **Email sequences** — build multi-step outreach campaigns with delays, personalization fields, and follow-ups
- **One-time emails** — send a single ad hoc email to a contact or table outside of a sequence
- **AI Email Assistant** — generate personalized email drafts based on your value proposition, the prospect's profile, and your chosen tone
- **Performance tracking** — monitor open rates, reply rates, and bounce rates per sequence
- **Campaign Health Score** — get AI-generated suggestions for improving sequence performance
- **Sending limits** — set daily send caps per mailbox to protect deliverability

## How it fits into your workflow

Engage sits at the end of the prospecting flow. You find contacts in Workspace or Search, reveal their details, then add them directly to a sequence — from Lusha Search, the Extension, or a Contact table. No CSV export, no copy-pasting into another tool.

<Note>
Engage requires a connected Gmail or Outlook mailbox. You connect your mailbox under **Engagement → Engage → Settings → Mailbox**.
</Note>

## Plan availability

Engage is available on all Lusha plans.

You're all set to start turning your prospect tables into active outreach — right from Lusha.
