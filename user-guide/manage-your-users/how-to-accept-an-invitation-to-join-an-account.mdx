---
title: "How to accept an invitation to join an account"
description: "How to accept an invitation to join an existing Lusha team account, whether or not you already have an account."
---

When your admin invites you to join a Lusha team account, you can accept the invitation from the email or directly from your dashboard.

## If you already have a Lusha account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your account">
    Log in to your [Lusha account](https://dashboard.lusha.com/dashboard).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the invitation">
    The invitation will appear as a pop-up or in the top-left corner of your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    Click **Accept invitation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap accounts to join the team">
    Click **Swap accounts** to complete the process and join the team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can also accept the invitation by clicking **Join the team** directly in the invitation email.
</Note>

## If you don't have a Lusha account yet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation link">
    Click the invitation link you received by email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Sign up">
    Select **Sign up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Enter your work email address, first name, last name, job title, and phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Get Started">
    Click **Get Started**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the verification code">
    Enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once verified, you'll be taken to your Lusha dashboard and automatically added to the team.

<Note>
If the invitation link takes you to the login page, don't create a new account - just log in with your existing email and password.
</Note>

You're all set and ready to start prospecting with your team.

To learn how to invite others to your account, see [How to invite a user to your plan](/user-guide/manage-your-users/how-to-invite-a-user-to-your-plan).
