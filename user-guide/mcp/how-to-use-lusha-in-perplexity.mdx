<Info>
**Availability:** The Lusha connector for Perplexity is rolling out to Perplexity's Connectors directory. If you don't see Lusha listed yet in your Perplexity account, check back soon - availability depends on Perplexity's own rollout schedule.
</Info>

Lusha is available as a native connector in Perplexity, giving Perplexity **Pro**, **Max**, and **Enterprise** users direct access to verified B2B contact and company data inside Search and Computer.

Once connected, you can ask Perplexity for people and companies in natural language - for example, prospecting, account research, or enrichment - and get verified phone numbers, emails, and company details back in-thread, without switching tools.

## Before you start, you will need

- A Perplexity account on a paid plan (Pro, Max, or Enterprise).
- A Lusha account with available credits.

<Note>
Connecting uses OAuth, the same sign-in flow as any other Lusha MCP client. You don't need an Admin or Manager role to connect your own account.
</Note>

## Connect Lusha to Perplexity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Perplexity Connectors">
    In Perplexity, go to your connectors settings and search for **Lusha**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Lusha">
    Click **Connect** next to the Lusha listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve permissions">
    Sign in to your Lusha account and approve the requested permissions. If you don't have a Lusha account yet, sign up before returning to Perplexity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start asking Perplexity for contacts and companies">
    Use Search or Computer as you normally would and include what you're looking for - Perplexity calls Lusha automatically when your request needs contact or company data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example requests

- "Find VPs of Sales at fintech companies in New York and give me their phone numbers."
- "Who is the CFO at Acme, and what's their email?"
- "Research this company and find the right marketing contact to reach out to."

## Credits and usage

Every request that reveals a phone number or email uses Lusha credits from your account's credit pool, the same as any other connected AI client.

Check your remaining balance any time at [Lusha Analytics](https://dashboard.lusha.com/insights), filtered by **Source → API** to see usage from Perplexity and other MCP clients.
