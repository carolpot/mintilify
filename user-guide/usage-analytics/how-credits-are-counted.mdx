Every time you reveal contact information in Lusha, credits are deducted from your balance. The exact cost depends on **what data you reveal** and **which feature you use**. This article covers every credit event across all Lusha features so there are no surprises.

<Warning>
Prices are subject to change across different pricing versions, per our [Terms & Conditions](https://www.lusha.com/legal/terms_and_conditions-2/).
</Warning>

## Complete credit cost table

Credits are charged based on the type of data revealed and the feature used. Use the table below as your reference.

| Feature | Action | Credits deducted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Platform & Extension** | Reveal email address | 1 credit |
| **Platform & Extension** | Reveal phone number | 5 credits |
| **Platform & Extension** | Reveal email + phone together | Up to 6 credits |
| **Platform & Extension** | Re-view an already-revealed contact | 0 credits |
| **Platform & Extension** | Search, filter, or view company info | 0 credits |
| **API** | Reveal email (per contact) | 1 credit |
| **API** | Reveal phone number (per contact) | 5 credits |
| **API** | Reveal company info (full profile) | 1 credit |
| **API** | Single API request (per result returned) | 1 credit per result |
| **API** | Bulk API request (up to 100 contacts/companies) | 1 credit per 1–25 results |
| **API** | API request with no results returned | 1 credit (minimum) |
| **API - Signals** | Company Signals | 1 credit per company |
| **API - Signals** | Contacts Signals | 1 credit per contact |
| **Workspace** | Contact or company reveal | Same as Platform rate |
| **Workspace** | AI chat prompts | 1 credit per 5 prompts |
| **Workspace** | AI Data enrichment | 1 credit per cell enriched |
| **Workspace** | Signals enrichment (signal found) | 1 credit per signal detected |
| **Workspace** | Signals enrichment (no signal found) | 0 credits |
| **Workspace** | CSV export | 1 credit per 25 rows |
| **Workspace** | CRM export (push to Salesforce / HubSpot) | 1 credit per export |
| **Workspace** | Lusha Data columns | 0 credits |
| **Workspace** | CRM columns (live Salesforce field values) | 0 credits |
| **CSV Enrichment** | Successfully enriched contact (matched) | Same as Platform rate |
| **CSV Enrichment** | Unmatched row (no data found) | 0 credits |
| **Recommendations table** | Reveal a blurred row (post-beta) | 1 credit per row |
| **Recommendations table** | Show contact email | 1 credit |
| **Recommendations table** | Show contact phone number | 5 credits |
| **Recommendations table** | Browsing / filtering / viewing the table | 0 credits |

<Note>
When you click **Reveal**, Lusha shows all available data for that contact at once. This is why a single reveal can deduct more than 1 credit - you're paying for each piece of contact data surfaced, not just one click.
</Note>

## How credits are deducted

Credits are charged based on the **types of data revealed** per contact, not per contact unlock. Each data point has a separate credit cost. The same contact's data point is only ever charged once - re-querying or re-viewing a contact you've already revealed will not incur additional charges for data already surfaced.

---

## API-specific rules

The API has a few additional rules that don't apply to the platform:

* **Minimum 1 credit per request** - even if no results are returned, 1 credit is consumed.
* **Bulk request pricing** - 1 credit covers up to 25 results. A 40-result bulk call costs 2 credits for the results, plus data point costs on top.
* **Company info via API costs 1 credit** - company data is free throughout the platform but charged via API.

<Note>
API access requires a key available to Admins and Managers on Premium and Scale plans. For full endpoint documentation, visit [docs.lusha.com](https://docs.lusha.com).
</Note>

## Workspace-specific rules

Workspace introduces credit events beyond standard reveals:

* **AI Data enrichment charges per cell** - a row with 3 AI Data columns costs 3 credits to enrich, not 1.
* **Signals enrichment only charges for populated cells** - if no signal is detected, that cell is free.
* **CSV export credits are separate from reveal credits** - exporting a table you've already revealed still costs 1 credit per 25 rows.
* **Signals are table-scoped** - running a signal search for the same contact in two different Workspace tables counts as two separate charges.

## What does NOT use a credit

Many actions in Lusha are completely free and do not touch your credit balance. You will **not** be charged for:

* **Searching and filtering** - browsing results, applying filters, and scrolling through the prospecting platform.
* **Viewing company information** - company names, industries, headcounts, and other firmographic data on the platform.
* **Re-viewing a contact you've already revealed** - the credit is only charged once per contact per cycle.
* **Saving contacts to a table** - adding a contact to a table without revealing their contact data.
* **Workspace Lusha Data and CRM columns** - these pull from verified data and live CRM fields at no cost.
* **Unmatched rows in CSV Enrichment** - rows Lusha cannot find a match for are always free.
* **Browsing the Recommendations table** - you're never charged just for viewing or filtering the table.

<Note>
Credits are only ever deducted when you click **Show details** (or **Reveal**) on a contact for the first time in a billing cycle, or when performing a paid Workspace action like AI Data enrichment or CSV export.
</Note>

## Why Analytics may show higher numbers than you expect

The Analytics dashboard reflects credits at the **account level**, across all users and all channels. A few things that can cause the number to look higher than expected:

* Credits used by **other users** on your account are included in the total.
* Credits consumed via **integrations** (Salesforce, HubSpot, API, etc.) count toward the balance.
* **Bulk reveals** deduct credits for all contacts in the selection, including those for whom only partial data was found.
* **Workspace actions** like AI Data enrichment and CSV exports add to the total beyond reveal-only usage.
* The **"This cycle"** view resets on your renewal date - not the calendar month - which can make the window feel shorter than expected.

---

## What are "conversation credits"?

Conversation credits are a separate credit type available on certain plan tiers. They are used when saving contact information through specific in-platform workflows. If you see this line item in your dashboard, it counts independently from your standard prospecting credits.

## If you believe there's a discrepancy in your credit count

1. Go to **Analytics → Credit Usage** and filter by user and date range.
2. Compare the breakdown against your known reveal activity - remember to account for Workspace actions (AI Data, Signals, CSV exports) and API usage.
3. If credits were deducted with no corresponding activity, contact Lusha Support via the chat icon in your dashboard.

<Warning>
**Important:** Credits cannot be refunded for reveals already made, even if the contact data was incomplete. If you regularly find data quality issues, let Lusha Support know - patterns are reviewed by the data team.
</Warning>
