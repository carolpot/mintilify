---
title: "How to set up automated usage report"
description: "Schedule recurring usage reports and send them to your team automatically from Account Settings."
---

Lusha can automatically send usage reports to you and your team on a regular schedule. This helps Admins and Managers track credit consumption, team activity, and seat usage without manually checking the Analytics dashboard.

<Note>
Automated usage reports are configured in **Account Settings → Report settings** tab. This tab is available to Admins only.
</Note>

## How to set up automated usage reports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Report settings">
    Go to **Account Settings** (/account) → **Report settings** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on automated reports">
    Toggle **Automated usage reports** to **On**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your frequency">
    Select your preferred frequency:

    - **Weekly** - sent on Mondays, covering the previous Mon–Fri
    - **Monthly** - sent on the 1st of each month, covering the previous month
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add recipient email addresses">
    Enter up to **5 recipient email addresses**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Changes save automatically">
    Changes save automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once enabled, the first report will be sent at the next scheduled cycle.

<Warning>
Recipient email addresses do not need to be Lusha users. Anyone you add will receive the report, so make sure you only add intended recipients.
</Warning>

Your team will now receive regular usage reports automatically - no manual pulls needed.
