---
title: "Auto Sequence"
description: "How to automatically enroll new contacts into Lusha Engage sequences the moment they're added to a table, using Auto Sequence."
---

Auto Sequence automatically enrolls new contacts into an email sequence the moment they're added to one of your tables - no manual steps needed. Only revealed contacts are enrolled, so you stay in control of credit usage.

<Note>
  When Auto Sequence runs, it also auto-reveals the contact's email address (Auto-Show). Each email reveal uses 1 credit.
</Note>

## How to set up Auto Sequence

1. Open a contact table and select the contacts you want to automate, or click **Add to sequence** in the top panel without selecting contacts to apply to all.

### Identifying contacts added via automation

1. Open the relevant sequence.
2. Go to the **Contacts** tab.
3. Look for the ⚡ icon next to a contact's name - this indicates they were enrolled via automation.

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/yu27vhse/44285570/496dd416ad750262c1c413aaa1ab/Screenshot+2025-03-31+at+14_25_13.png?expires=1780056000&signature=42cf117fde15bebbbfc79f05e5621a529ee56d7b512ccddfbd43431f5f69366a&req=1NFtzV3%2Fqzdk2hj99dpg6q4c9y7dMoGQuASFtYqj67WcmKNMDy9pXwYOMAgY%0A25CJwpAxUcGX6YS3jOVfIh77%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}} />

**What happens when you edit or delete a sequence linked to automation?**

- **Deleting** a linked sequence deactivates the automation.
- **Deactivating** a sequence does not pause the automation - contacts continue to be added, but emails won't send.
- **Discarding** a sequence (moving it to trash) deactivates the automation.

## How to disable an automation

Click the ⚡ lightning icon in the top-right corner of the table, then use the on/off toggle to disable the automation for that table.

## FAQs

**Will Lusha send emails without my approval?**\
No. Emails are only sent after you activate the sequence. Once active, enrolled contacts follow your sequence schedule.

**Can I use different sequences for different tables?**\
Yes - each table can have its own Auto Sequence linked to a different email sequence.

**Is there a limit on automations per table?**\
Yes. You can set one Auto Sequence automation per table. It applies to all new contacts added to that table.

**Can I turn off automation for specific tables?**\
Yes. All automations are managed per table and can be toggled off independently.

**What if I edit a sequence linked to automation?**\
You can edit the sequence freely. The automation will continue using the updated version unless the sequence is deleted or discarded.
