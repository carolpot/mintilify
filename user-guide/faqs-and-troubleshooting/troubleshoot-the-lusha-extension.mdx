---
title: "Troubleshoot the Lusha extension"
description: "How to fix the most common issues with the Lusha Chrome and Edge extension"
---

If the Lusha Extension isn't showing contact data, won't open, or is behaving unexpectedly, you can usually fix it in a few minutes. This guide covers the most common extension issues and how to resolve each one.

## Possible causes

- You're not signed in to the extension
- The extension needs to be updated
- A browser conflict or cache issue is preventing the extension from loading
- The page you're on isn't supported
- Your account has run out of credits
- Corrupted extension storage is causing a persistent reload loop

## Fix 1: Make sure you're signed in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the Lusha icon">
    Click the Lusha icon in your browser toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in if prompted">
    If you see a **Sign In** button, click it and enter your Lusha credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh and retry">
    Once signed in, refresh the page and try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix 2: Update the extension

An outdated extension can cause display issues or data not loading.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chrome extensions">
    In Chrome, go to **chrome://extensions** in your address bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Developer mode">
    Enable **Developer mode** (top-right toggle).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Force-update extensions">
    Click **Update** to force-update all extensions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the page">
    Refresh the page you're prospecting on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In Edge, go to **edge://extensions** and follow the same steps.

## Fix 3: Clear the extension cache

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage extension">
    Right-click the Lusha icon in your toolbar and click **Manage extension**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the extension off and on">
    Toggle the extension **off**, wait 5 seconds, then toggle it back **on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the page">
    Refresh the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If that doesn't help:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open browsing data settings">
    Go to **chrome://settings/clearBrowserData**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select what to clear">
    Select **Cached images and files** and **Cookies and other site data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the data">
    Click **Clear data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reopen your browser and retry">
    Reopen your browser and try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix 4: Check if the page is supported

The Lusha Extension works on:

- LinkedIn (profiles and search results)
- Company websites
- Gmail (via Lusha Everywhere)
- Salesforce and HubSpot (via Lusha Everywhere)

The extension does not work on:

- Local files
- Chrome internal pages (e.g., chrome://settings)
- Pages that block extensions via Content Security Policy

If the Lusha panel isn't opening on a specific page, try visiting the person's LinkedIn profile directly.

## Fix 5: Check your credit balance

If you can open the extension but can't reveal contacts, you may have run out of credits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Usage">
    Go to [dashboard.lusha.com](https://dashboard.lusha.com) → **Settings → Usage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your remaining credits">
    Check your **credits remaining**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait or ask your Admin">
    If you're out of credits, wait for your next billing cycle or ask your Admin to adjust your allocation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle. Your Admin can check team-wide usage under **Analytics**.
</Note>

## Fix 6: Reinstall the extension

If none of the above works, a clean reinstall usually resolves persistent issues.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove the extension">
    Right-click the Lusha icon → **Remove from Chrome**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Lusha in the Chrome Web Store">
    Go to the Chrome Web Store and search for **Lusha**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reinstall and sign in">
    Click **Add to Chrome** and sign back in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix 7: Extension only works after a page reload

If the extension shows contact data after a reload but stops responding as you navigate between LinkedIn profiles - requiring a fresh reload each time - extension storage is likely corrupted or the auto-open setting has been turned off.

**Step 1: Check your auto-open setting**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the extension on LinkedIn">
    Open the Lusha extension on LinkedIn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the More tab">
    Click the **More** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Auto-open is on">
    Make sure **Auto-open** is toggled **on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test on another profile">
    Navigate to another LinkedIn profile to test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Step 2: Clear extension storage**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chrome extensions">
    Go to **chrome://extensions** in your address bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Lusha's details">
    Find **Lusha** and click **Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear extension storage">
    Scroll down to **Extension storage** and click **Clear site data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload and test">
    Reload LinkedIn and open the extension to test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If clearing storage doesn't resolve it, go back to Fix 6 to do a clean reinstall.

If the issue still isn't resolved after reinstalling, contact **Lusha Support** using the chat icon in your dashboard. Let us know your browser version and which page the issue occurs on.
