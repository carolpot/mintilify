---
title: "Why Lusha asks for permissions to read and change data across all websites"
description: "Why the Lusha extension requests permission to read and change data across all websites, and what that enables."
---

When you install the Lusha extension, Chrome prompts you to allow Lusha to "read and change your data on all websites." This permission is what makes Lusha work on LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, your CRM, and any other website you use for prospecting.

Lusha only reads page data when you actively open the extension. We don't store or sell your browsing data. You can read the full details in the [Lusha Privacy Policy](https://www.lusha.com/legal/privacy_notice/).

## What this permission enables

### Lusha on LinkedIn

Use the extension directly on LinkedIn profiles, company pages, Sales Navigator, and LinkedIn Recruiter. Reveal contact details and bulk enrich prospect tables without leaving LinkedIn.

### Lusha Everywhere

Use the extension on any company website - not just LinkedIn. Browse to a company's website, click the Lusha icon, and see a table of employees and their contact information.

### Lusha on CRM

Use the extension inside Salesforce and HubSpot to enrich contact profiles and account pages directly within your CRM - no switching between tabs.

You're all set - granting this permission gives you the full Lusha extension experience across the web.
