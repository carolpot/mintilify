---
title: "How to update your Lusha extension"
description: "How to manually update the Lusha extension to the latest version by reinstalling from the Chrome Web Store."
---

The Lusha extension updates automatically in Chrome. But if you're seeing outdated behaviour or missing features, manually reinstalling the extension gets you the latest version immediately.

## How to update the Lusha extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chrome extensions">
    Open Chrome and go to **chrome://extensions** in your address bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Lusha">
    Find **Lusha** in your extension table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the extension">
    Click **Remove** to uninstall it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Use the Remove button - do not just toggle the extension off. The extension must be fully removed for the update to work.
</Warning>

### Reinstall the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the latest version">
    Go to the [Lusha Chrome Store page](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/lusha-easily-find-b2b-con/mcebeofpilippmndlpcghpmghcljajna) and click **Add to Chrome** to install the latest version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear your browser cache">
    Clear your browser cache: press **Ctrl + Shift + Del** (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + Delete** (Mac), or go to **chrome://settings** and clear browsing data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart Chrome and confirm">
    Restart Chrome and navigate to a LinkedIn profile to confirm the extension is working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You're all set - the extension is now running the latest version.
