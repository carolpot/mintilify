---
title: "Why is my extension blank on Chrome?"
description: "How to fix a blank or grey Lusha extension on Chrome by adjusting your cookie settings."
---

If your Lusha extension is showing as blank or displaying only grey lines with no content, the cause is almost always a cookie setting in Chrome that's blocking the extension from loading.

## Fix: Allow third-party cookies in Chrome

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chrome cookie settings">
    Open Chrome and go to **chrome://settings/cookies** in your address bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the current setting">
    Check which option is currently selected under the general cookies setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow the needed cookies">
    Select either **Allow all cookies** or **Block third-party cookies in Incognito**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart Chrome and test">
    Close and reopen Chrome, then visit a LinkedIn profile to test the extension.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Lusha is a third-party app and requires third-party cookies to function. If cookies are fully blocked, the extension will appear blank.
</Note>

## If the issue persists

Try updating your extension to the latest version - see [Update your Lusha extension](/user-guide/faqs-and-troubleshooting/how-to-update-your-lusha-extension) for steps.

If the problem continues after updating, contact **Lusha Support** using the chat icon in your dashboard.
