---
title: "Why is Lusha extension asking me to log in when I'm already logged in?"
description: "How to fix the Lusha extension sign-in loop with cookie settings and browser fixes."
---

If the Lusha extension is showing a **Log in** prompt even though you're already signed in, a cookie or browser session issue is usually the cause. Work through the fixes below in order.

## Fix 1: Allow all cookies in Chrome

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chrome cookie settings">
    Go to **chrome://settings/cookies** in your Chrome address bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow all cookies">
    Make sure **Allow all cookies** is selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh and retry">
    Refresh the LinkedIn page and try opening the extension again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix 2: Sign in via the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the Lusha icon">
    Click the Lusha icon in your toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Log in">
    Click **Log in** - you'll be redirected to the Lusha dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your Lusha credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to LinkedIn">
    Return to LinkedIn and refresh the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix 3: Update and restart Chrome

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install available updates">
    Go to **chrome://settings/help** and install any available updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart Chrome">
    Fully close and reopen Chrome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in again">
    Sign in to the extension again with your Lusha credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix 4: Reset Chrome settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chrome reset settings">
    Go to **chrome://settings/reset**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore default settings">
    Click **Restore settings to their original defaults** and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign back in">
    Sign back in to the extension.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix 5: Restart your computer

If none of the above work, restart your computer and sign in to the extension again.

If the issue still isn't resolved, contact **Lusha Support** using the chat icon in your dashboard or email [support@lusha.com](mailto:support@lusha.com).
