---
title: "Cancel Lusha extension auto-open"
description: "How to stop the Lusha extension from opening automatically on LinkedIn profile pages by disabling the auto-open setting."
---

By default, the Lusha extension opens automatically each time you visit a LinkedIn profile. If you'd prefer it to stay minimised, you can disable this in your extension settings.

## How to disable auto-open

In the Lusha extension, toggle off **Auto-open plugin**.

When auto-open is off, the Lusha panel won't open automatically on profile pages. Instead, only the Lusha icon will appear on the right side of the screen. If contact data is found, a number will appear on the icon showing how many details are available. Click the icon to open the panel.

<img src="https://lusha-eu.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/13259948/c421b9611308e330068360a5/Screen%2BShot%2B2021-03-08%2Bat%2B8_56_53.png?expires=1780056000&signature=9176b6de8ffd8d7a5b3b9176c24de5dc284525d72ca602c7d5fe5ad1ca4c8cb6&req=0dZtwFHzqD9k2hj99dpg6gU3byNIEHzt6SGvbyqrtQZJCoOZPaXV4QKWHiej%0ANl3J9qTlcPZftxkhH74pG0zv%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}} />

You can also move the Lusha icon up or down on the screen by dragging the six-dot handle at the bottom of the panel.

<img src="https://lusha-eu.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/13259985/5f6c4c0c043c5184a92736ef/Screen%2BShot%2B2021-03-08%2Bat%2B9_00_40.png?expires=1780056000&signature=4fc0feed2bfb94ca064a3b5fd5c0a98b1026638128441086707f810fd4c72a84&req=0dZtwFHzpDJk2hj99dpg6vezJyWGucuOb2po4U65C7kOc83YfoumC2klbZJo%0A%2FyTxgCOa%2BHiL0GdNKBVK8Tvp%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}} />

You're all set — the extension will now stay minimised until you choose to open it.
