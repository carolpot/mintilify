---
title: "How to Create a Target Profile"
description: "Learn how to set up a Target Profile in ICP Hub so your AI recommendations match the companies and contacts you actually want to reach."
---

A Target Profile tells Lusha's recommendation engine who your ideal customers are. Once saved, it runs continuously in the background and shapes the contacts and companies surfaced in your recommendation lists. You can create up to 10 Target Profiles per user.

## Before You Start

- You need a Lusha account on any plan (Free, Pro, Premium, or Scale).
- To use CRM-based exclusions, your account Admin must first connect and consent to a CRM integration in ICP Hub. See **[ICP Hub: How AI Recommendations Work](https://info.lusha.com/en/articles/662006-icp-hub-how-ai-recommendations-work)** for details.

<Tip>
Already searching in Workspace? If you've built a search using ICP-supported filters, click the **Set as ICP** button in the search panel to pre-populate a Target Profile with your current filters. You can then add personas, adjust preferences, and save — without starting from scratch.
</Tip>

## Step 1: Open ICP Hub

1. **Log in** to your Lusha account.
2. Open the **Profile dropdown** at the top right and select **ICP Hub**.
3. Click **+ New ICP** or, if this is your first time, follow the onboarding prompt to define your first profile.

<Note>
If you haven't set up an ICP yet, you'll already have a feed on your Home page — labeled "Lusha generated" — based on your account history. Creating a Target Profile replaces this with a scored feed tailored to your actual targets.
</Note>

## Step 2: Name Your Target Profile

1. Enter a name for this profile in the **Profile Name** field.
2. Choose a name that reflects the segment you're targeting — for example, "Mid-Market SaaS – VP Sales" or "Enterprise EMEA".

## Step 3: Set Your Account Criteria

Account Criteria define the types of companies you want to reach.

1. Under **Inclusion Rules → Account Criteria**, add the filters that describe your target accounts.

Supported filters include:

- HQ company location
- Industry and sub-industry
- Specialties and keywords
- Employee headcount
- Revenue

2. Each filter you add is applied as a **Must Include** criterion — a strict filter that excludes companies that don't match.

## Step 4: Set Your Persona Criteria

Persona Criteria define the contacts — the specific buyers or decision-makers — within your target accounts.

1. Under **Target Audience**, define the attributes for your first persona.

![Persona criteria configuration](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/yu27vhse/138303128/4b829101c255c38dc863e307b95b/2204dfa8-8145-43a6-8ef1-0f3fe8963ebd)

Supported filters include:

- Job title
- Department
- Seniority
- Contact location
- Contact details (e.g., has direct dial, has email)

2. To add a second buyer persona (e.g., both VP of Sales and Head of Marketing), click **Add Persona**. You can add up to 4 personas per Target Profile. Multiple personas are treated as OR logic — a contact matching any one persona is eligible.

3. Each filter you add is applied as a **Must Include** criterion — contacts that don't match are excluded.

## Step 5: Review Your Potential Population

As you build your Target Profile, Lusha displays the estimated number of contacts and companies that match your Must Include criteria.

- If the count is rounded to 0 (below 100), your criteria are too narrow. Relax one or more Must Include filters before saving.
- If the count shows in the thousands, your targeting is broad — consider tightening your criteria.

<Warning>
The preview shows a limited set of matching contacts and companies so you can verify relevance before saving.
</Warning>

## Step 6: Configure Exclusion Rules (Optional)

Exclusion Rules prevent certain contacts or companies from appearing in your recommendations.

1. Under **Exclusion Rules**, choose what to exclude for contacts:
   - **Shown contacts** — exclude contacts already shown to you, to any user, or no one (default: shown by me).
   - **Saved contacts** — exclude contacts already saved (default: none).

2. Choose what to exclude for companies:
   - **Companies with shown contacts** — exclude companies where a threshold of contacts has already been revealed.
   - **Saved companies** — exclude companies already saved.

3. To exclude based on CRM records, select the CRM objects you want to use as exclusion criteria. Available objects depend on your connected CRM:
   - **Salesforce:** Lead, Contact, Opportunity, Account
   - **HubSpot:** Contacts, Companies, Deals

<Warning>
CRM-based exclusions are only available if your account Admin has connected a CRM and provided consent in ICP Hub. If you see a Connect CRM prompt, reach out to your admin.
</Warning>

**Supported CRMs:** Salesforce, HubSpot. Coming soon: Pipedrive, Zoho, Bullhorn, and Outreach. Click **Notify me** on the coming-soon CRM card to join the waitlist.

To revoke a permission, go to **Integrations → Manage** and disable **CRM-based AI recommendations**.

## Step 7: Add Intent Topics (Optional)

If your account has intent topics configured, you can include them in your Target Profile to boost recommendations from companies showing buying signals.

Under **Intent**, select the relevant intent topics for this profile.

If no intent topics are configured for your account yet, click the prompt to set them up. Intent topics act as ranking boosters in AI recommendations — they don't exclude non-matching results, and they are not applied when using a Target Profile as a search filter.

## Step 8: Save Your Target Profile

Review your configuration.

Click **Save**.

Once saved, your Target Profile is active. Lusha's recommendation engine will begin using it to generate and refresh your recommendation lists.

<Note>
Your saved Target Profile also becomes available as a filter preset in the Workspace search panel. You'll find it under the ICP section in the filter panel — shared profiles appear for all users on the account, private profiles appear only for you.
</Note>

<Note>
Check your Home page the following day to see your updated feed. The Signals column shows you exactly why each lead surfaced based on your new profile.
</Note>

## Managing Your Target Profiles

From the ICP Hub, you can manage all your Target Profiles:

- **Edit** — modify any aspect of an existing profile.
- **Duplicate** — copy a profile to use as a starting point for a new segment. The copy is saved with the same name plus "copy".
- **Deactivate** — temporarily pause a profile. Deactivated profiles don't generate recommendations but can be reactivated later.
- **Delete** — permanently remove a profile. You'll be asked to confirm if the profile is linked to any active configuration.

<Note>
Admins can share their Target Profiles with all users in the account. Shared profiles appear as read-only for non-admin users, but can be selected and used. Non-admins can create their own profiles and disable the shared profile if they want.
</Note>

You're all set! Your Target Profile is now live and guiding your AI recommendations. Check your recommendation lists within 24 hours to see your results.
