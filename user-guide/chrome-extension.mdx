The Lusha browser extension lets you prospect wherever you work - LinkedIn, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, company websites, your CRM, Gmail, and other B2B sites - without switching tabs. It's available for Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

## Install the extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Chrome Web Store">
    Search for "Lusha Extension" in the Chrome Web Store, or use the direct listing link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Chrome">
    Click **Add to Chrome**, then confirm by clicking **Add extension**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the privacy notice">
    Review the privacy policy pop-up and click **Got it, let's go** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Click the Lusha icon in your browser toolbar and log in with your Lusha account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The same extension is available for Microsoft Edge from the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store - search for "Lusha" and click **Get**.
</Info>

## Using the extension

Log in, then navigate to LinkedIn, a company website, or your CRM. Click the blue Lusha icon on the side of the screen to open the extension panel. The extension works across LinkedIn profiles, LinkedIn search results, Sales Navigator, and company pages, revealing contact and company details based on your subscription tier.

From the extension panel you can:

- **Save to a table** - store a contact in an existing or new Workspace table, with optional tags.
- **Export to CRM** - send a lead, contact, or account record directly to Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive.
- **Add to a sequence** - add contacts to a Lusha Engage email sequence (up to 150 contacts at a time from the extension).
- **Search** - query Lusha's prospecting database from the extension's Search tab, without leaving the page you're on.

The extension also has an **Activity** tab that lists your saved contacts with filtering and bulk actions, and a **More** tab for quick access to account information and platform shortcuts.

## Viewing saved contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the extension">
    Click the Lusha icon on LinkedIn, your CRM, or any website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Activity tab">
    Click the Activity icon at the bottom of the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Saved tab">
    Switch to the **Saved** tab at the top of the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a table">
    Pick the relevant table from the dropdown to see its contacts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From the Saved tab, click the three dots next to any contact to send an email, export to your CRM, or move the contact to a different table. Clicking a contact's name opens their full profile, including direct dials, emails, and LinkedIn details.

## Preferences

The extension includes a few customizable settings, such as which side of the screen the panel opens on, whether it auto-opens on supported sites, and "Lusha Everywhere," which surfaces Lusha data on general B2B websites in addition to LinkedIn and your CRM.

<Tip>
  If contact data isn't loading, check that cookies are enabled for the extension (`chrome://settings/cookies` → **Allow all cookies**), then log out and back in.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Using the Lusha extension doesn't automate browsing on LinkedIn and doesn't violate LinkedIn's terms of service - it won't put your LinkedIn account at risk.
</Note>
