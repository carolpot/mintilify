title: "Moving from Classic tables to Workspaces"
description: "Learn how Lusha is transitioning your contact and company tables into a more powerful table-based workspace."
---

We are upgrading the way you organize your data by moving from classic tables to **Workspace Tables**. This change provides a more flexible, table-based interface for managing your prospects and introduces AI-driven intelligence to your workflow. In this article, you'll learn what to expect during this transition and how Lusha's AI helps you grow your pipeline automatically.

This transition applies to all Lusha plans: Existing **Free**, **Pro**, **Premium**, and specific **Scale accounts** who users one wallet credit system.

<Note>
**Not using Workspace (Legacy Scale users)?** If you save contacts from the Lusha extension or prospecting platform, **Tables is simply the new name for tables**. Nothing about how you work has changed - you'll just see "Tables" where you used to see "tables".
</Note>

## The Power of AI in Your Workspace

Moving to **Workspace Tables** isn't just a design update- it's a move toward smarter prospecting. Your tables now act as a living data source for Lusha's AI.

- **Pattern Recognition:** As you save contacts to a table, Lusha's AI analyzes the common traits of your prospects (such as industry, seniority, and company size).
- **Smart Recommendations:** Based on the data in your tables, the AI surfaces "lookalike" leads- high-potential prospects that match your successful patterns.
- **Continuous Learning:** The more you reveal and save to your **Workspace Tables**, the smarter the recommendations become, effectively building a self-growing pipeline.

## Key changes to your workflow

- **Automatic Transition:** Any action you take on a classic table- such as opening it or saving a new contact- will automatically trigger a migration to a **Workspace Table**.
- **Consolidated View:** Instead of navigating through separate menus for contacts and companies, you will now access all saved data through your **Workspaces**.
- **Table Entry Points:** You can now create and access new tables directly from a dedicated entry point in your main navigation bar, or from your home page.

## How it works

When you reveal a contact in the **Prospecting Platform**, the **Lusha Extension**, or on a **Company Page**, the **Save** action will now prompt you to select or create a **Workspace Table**.

- **Saving Data:** When you click **Save**, your contacts are instantly added to your table.
- **Viewing Employees:** If you click to view employees from a company table, Lusha will now show those results within a filtered table view rather than redirecting you back to the general **Prospecting** page.
- **Automations:** Your existing active automations will continue to run.

<Note>
While you can still manage your current active automations, the ability to create ***new*** *automations is temporarily blocked during this transition period.*
</Note>

## Feature Adjustments

To improve performance within the new table structure, some legacy features have been adjusted:

- **Enhanced CSV Exports**: When you export from a Workspace Table, the CSV will now contain all available data columns- significantly more than the classic table export. The shown columns in the workspace will be the ones that will be exported. Learn how to adjust the columns [here](/user-guide/introduction-and-getting-started/workspace-ai-powered-table-building).

<Note>
If you require the original, simplified export format, you can still access it by clicking the three dots (⋮) next to the Table name and selecting the legacy export option.
</Note>

- **Tags:** Tags are now supported in Workspace Tables. You can tag contacts directly from within a saved table, and tags added through the Lusha Extension or Prospecting are reflected automatically. Tags are included in CSV and CRM exports.
- **All Companies:** The **All Companies** tab in the navigation bar has been removed. You can now find all your saved company data directly within your dedicated tables.

Now you're ready to prospect smarter.
