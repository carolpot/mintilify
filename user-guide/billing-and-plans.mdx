Lusha runs on a unified credit system that spans the web app, the browser extension, the API, and other tools. Credits are your currency for revealing contact data and using certain platform features.

## How credits work

You spend credits when you reveal contact information — for example, an email or phone number. Searching and filtering don't cost credits, and re-viewing a contact you've already revealed is free.

Typical credit costs include:

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Reveal an email | 1 credit |
| Reveal a phone number | 5 credits |
| Reveal both email and phone | Up to 6 credits |
| Re-view an already-revealed contact | 0 credits |
| Search or filter | 0 credits |

Credits are grouped into broader categories under Lusha's pricing system: essential prospecting (verified contact and company data), workflow automations, sales intelligence (signals), and client engagement tools.

## Credit renewal and rollover

- **Monthly plans** — unused credits roll over to the next month, up to a cap of 2× your monthly allowance.
- **Annual plans** — your full year of credits is allocated upfront, with no monthly reset.
- **Free plans** — unused credits don't roll over.

Credits reset on your billing anniversary date, not necessarily the first of the month. You can confirm your exact renewal date under **Settings → Payments & Subscription**.

## Plan tiers

Lusha's plans differ mainly in seat limits and available features:

| Plan | Seats |
|---|---|
| Starter | 1 |
| Pro | 2 seats included, expandable up to 10 |
| Premium | 5 seats included, expandable up to 25 |
| Scale | Unlimited seats |

<Note>
  The Manager role, credit limits per user or group, inactivity timeouts, and SSO configuration are available on Scale plans only.
</Note>

## Managing payments and subscription

Admins and Managers can view invoice history, payment methods, current plan details, credit balance, and renewal dates from **Settings → Payments & Subscription**. Enterprise customers should contact their Account Manager for plan changes instead of upgrading through the pricing page.

## Upgrading your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account settings">
    Click your initials in the left navigation, then go to **Account & settings → Payments & Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change Plan">
    This opens Lusha's pricing page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new plan">
    Choose the plan that fits your team. Lusha calculates a prorated credit for the unused portion of your current plan, shown at checkout as a "Refund for unused plan" line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Upgrading starts a new billing cycle. Credits from your previous plan don't carry over.
</Warning>

## Downgrading, freezing, or canceling

You can also downgrade your plan, move from a legacy plan to a current one, freeze your subscription temporarily, or cancel outright. Each of these affects your credits and data differently, so review the details in account settings before making a change, or contact Lusha support if you're unsure.
