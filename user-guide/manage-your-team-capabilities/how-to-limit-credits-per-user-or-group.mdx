---
title: "How to limit credits per user or group"
description: "How to set credit limits per user or per group in Lusha, and how to monitor your team's credit usage."
---

Admins and Managers on Scale plans can set credit limits per user or per group. This lets you control how many credits each team member or team group can use within a billing cycle.

<Note>
This feature is available on Scale plans and selected self-service plans only.
</Note>

## Step 1: Set your credit limit cycle

Before setting individual limits, choose whether limits reset monthly or annually.

1. Go to **[Account Settings](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account/accountSettings)**.
2. Under the credit limit section, select **Monthly** or **Annual**.
3. Save your selection.

## How to set a credit limit per user

1. Go to **Team Management → All Members**.
2. Click on the user row — a side panel will open.
3. Click the **pencil icon** next to the credit limit field.
4. Enter a limit (between 0 and 98,000).
5. Click **Save**.

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/16585924/6db2f4cb6884b260100ee8fd/Screenshot+2024-07-09+at+12_49_11.png?expires=1780056000&signature=6e7598221c258e6960abbe77f44e3e8fcee0184e9b7a44f3e2555a438c47b56b&req=0dNqzV3zrjNk2hj99dpg6hPK8isbz3sZfPyo91YFJ4dEGyRshXqIjO68DGyD%0A0vNYz5ZehgXNYudJqov%2BrR3g%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

To set limits for multiple users at once:

1. Check the boxes next to the users you want to update.
2. Click **Set credit limit** in the upper toolbar.
3. Enter the limit and click **Save**.

## How to set a credit limit per group

1. Go to **Team Management → Groups**.
2. Select the group you want to limit.
3. Click **Set credit limit** in the upper toolbar — a side panel will open.
4. Click the **pencil icon** and enter the limit.
5. Click **Save**.

<Note>
A group limit is shared across all members of the group. For example, if a group of 3 users has a combined limit of 500 credits, they can collectively use up to 500 credits **in the cycle** - not 500 each.
</Note>

## Monitoring credit usage

The Team Management table uses colour indicators to show each member's credit status at a glance:

- **Green** — credits available
- **Orange** — near limit (75% used)
- **Red** — limit reached

<Note>
Credit limits set by admins are visible to all team members in the **Team Management** tab, so users can see their own and others limit, as well as their current usage at any time.
</Note>

A coloured notification dot also appears in the navigation bar when a team member reaches 75% (orange) or 100% (red) of their limit.

Use the **status filter** in Team Management to sort members by their current credit usage status.

You're all set - credit limits are now active and will reset automatically at the start of each cycle.
