---
title: "How to change roles within your account"
description: "How to change a team member's role between User, Manager, and Admin in Lusha."
---

You can change any team member's role — User, Manager, or Admin — from the Team Management section of your dashboard. Role changes can be made individually or in bulk.

<Note>
Only Admins can change roles. The Manager role is available on Scale plans only.
</Note>

## Role types

- **User** - access to credits and Lusha's core features. Counts as a seat.
- **Admin** - access to credits and full admin rights. Counts as a seat.
- **Manager** - admin rights but no access to credits. Does not count as a seat. (Scale plan only.)

## How to change a team member's role

1. Go to **[Team Management](https://dashboard.lusha.com/team)** in your dashboard.
2. Select the user or users whose role you want to change.
3. Click **Change role**.
4. Select the new role.
5. Click **Save**.

If you're on a single-user plan and need to change a role, contact Lusha Support using the chat icon in your dashboard or email [support@lusha.com](mailto:support@lusha.com).
