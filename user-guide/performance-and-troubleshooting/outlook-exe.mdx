## Error Handling

- In case you face issues downloading the exe file, you can switch back to [connect using our web SSO](/user-guide/getting-started/join-lusha-community) (oAuth web process), Gmail/Outlook, depending on your email provider.
- In case there is a technical issue to collect the mailbox data, you will get an indication of the reason for the issue. For example: using a different email ID than the one you have registered to Lusha with, missing contacts data, etc.

  If you cannot resolve the issue, have any additional questions, or just want to talk feel free to contact our [Support team](mailto:support@lusha.com).

- The current version supports Outlook installation for Windows only.

## FAQs

**Q: Do I have to have Outlook on my PC?**

A: Yes, we just replace our outlook exe with the existing one. In case you don't have Outlook installed on your windows, then use the web SSO options to connect and join the community.

**Q: Can I use Outlook for MAC?**

A: The current version supports Outlook installation for Windows only.

**Q: Can I use 2 different devices (PC's) to connect with Outlook for desktop?**

**A:** No. Only one device is applicable.

**Q: Do I have to be a Lusha user to join?**

**A:** Yes. Otherwise, you will fail the authentication process. You cannot sign up for Lusha through the Outlook installation process. [Register to Lusha](https://dashboard.lusha.com/signup) and then join Lusha's community.

**Q: What information does Lusha collect from my Outlook email account?**

A: The information that Lusha collects is the same business information that can be found on a business card, including Full name, company name, job title, business phone number, and email address.

The majority of information that we collect from Lusha Community members confirms the data we already have in our database for professional purposes and acts as a system of validation and fraud protection.

**Q: Will I get any indication that I've successfully connected my Outlook and joined the Lusha Community?**

**A:** Yes. at the end of the installation and verification process, you will get a message that you've completed the process. In addition, once you return to the Lusha dashboard you get an indication of the credits you received.
