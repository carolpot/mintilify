---
title: "Out of office auto-reply in Lusha Engage"
description: "How Lusha Engage handles out-of-office auto-replies and how to resume paused contacts in a sequence."
---

When a contact's out-of-office auto-reply is detected, Lusha Engage automatically pauses that contact in the sequence. This prevents your emails from landing while they're away. You can resume the sequence once they're back.

## How to find paused contacts

## View by sequence

1. Go to the [Engage dashboard](https://dashboard.lusha.com/engage). Sequences with paused contacts show an orange clock icon.
2. Click the sequence with the orange clock icon.
3. Go to the **Contacts** tab.
4. Under the **Paused** filter, select **Auto-replied**.

## View affected emails

1. Click on the sequence with the orange clock icon.
2. Go to the **Emails** tab.
3. Filter by **Auto-Replied**.
4. Look for the orange **Auto-Replied** tag next to the affected messages.

## How to resume a paused contact

1. Check the contact's return date from the auto-reply email in your mailbox.
2. On the day they're back, go to the **Contacts** tab in the relevant sequence.
3. Tick the box next to the contact's name.
4. Click **Activate** to resume email sending to that contact immediately.
