title: "How to cancel your Lusha subscription"
description: "How to cancel your Lusha subscription, what happens to your data and credits, and how to request a refund."
---

You can cancel your Lusha subscription at any time from your account settings. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing cycle, after which your account moves to the free plan.

<Note>
Only Admins can cancel a subscription. If you're on a Scale plan, contact your Account Manager - cancellations for Scale plans take effect at the end of your subscription term.
</Note>

## Before you cancel - things to know

- Your data remains accessible after cancellation, but CSV export is a paid feature - export your [Lusha tables](https://dashboard.lusha.com/contact-tables) before cancelling.
- You'll keep access to your remaining credits until the end of the current cycle.
- All stored credits reset when your plan downgrades to free.
- Future plans may be priced differently from your current one.
- If you have multiple emails linked to the same subscription, the cancellation applies to all of them.

## How to cancel your subscription

1. Log in to your [Lusha dashboard](https://dashboard.lusha.com/) and click your name in the left navigation.
2. Go to **Account & Settings → [Payment & Subscription](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account/subscription)**.
3. Click **Cancel Plan**.

## Changed your mind?

You can undo the cancellation at any time before the end of your billing cycle. Go to **Payment & Subscription** and click **Revert Cancellation**.

## Cancelling vs. deleting your account

Cancelling your subscription and deleting your account are two different things. Here's what each one does:

| **Action** | **What it does** |
| --- | --- |
| **Cancel subscription** | Stops future billing. Your account moves to the free plan at the end of the cycle. Your saved data, contacts, and tables remain accessible. |
| **Delete account** | Permanently removes your account and all associated data from Lusha. This cannot be undone. |

If you want your account and personal data removed entirely — not just your subscription stopped — you need to request account deletion separately. Cancelling your subscription alone does not delete your account or data.

<Warning>
Account deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to all saved contacts, tables, and export history. Export your data before submitting a deletion request.
</Warning>

## To delete a paid account

1. Cancel your subscription first using the steps above. Your account will move to the free plan at the end of your billing cycle.
2. Once on the free plan, contact Support via the chat icon in your dashboard or email [support@lusha.com](mailto:support@lusha.com) to request account deletion.

## To delete a free account

Contact Support directly via the chat icon in your dashboard or email [support@lusha.com](mailto:support@lusha.com). Once deleted, you will no longer have access to any saved data.

<Note>
If you want to remove your personal contact details from Lusha's database (for example, if your phone number appears in Lusha's prospecting data), that's a separate data removal request. See [How to remove your details from Lusha](/user-guide/privacy-and-compliance/how-to-remove-your-details-from-lusha).
</Note>
