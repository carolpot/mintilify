---
title: "Why was my payment declined?"
description: "Common reasons why a Lusha payment is declined and how to resolve each one, plus how to reactivate a suspended account after a failed payment."
---

When a payment is declined, your account may be suspended until the issue is resolved. This article covers the most common reasons for declined payments, how to fix each one, and how to reactivate your account once payment goes through.

<Note>
  Accounts that are due for renewal but have a payment issue will be suspended until the issue is resolved. To update your billing details, see [Update your billing information](/user-guide/billing-and-payments/how-to-update-your-billing-information).
</Note>

## Insufficient funds

Your card doesn't have enough funds to cover the charge. Add funds to the card or try a different payment method.

## Transaction not allowed

Your card issuer has declined the transaction. This can happen if the card isn't enabled for purchases in USD - all Lusha plans are charged in USD. Contact your card issuer for details, or try a different payment method.

## Do not honor

Your card issuer declined without providing a reason. Contact your issuer for more information or try a different card. Retrying may succeed.

## Try again later / failed due to technical issue

This is a generic error. Try again later or use a different card.

## Incorrect number

The card number, expiry date, or CVV were entered incorrectly. Try again with the correct details.

## High risk / excessive retries

The same card has been used more than 15 times and is temporarily blocked by our payment processor. Try a different card, or wait at least 30 days before attempting with the same card again.

## My account is suspended - how do I reactivate it?

If your account has been suspended because a renewal payment failed, you can reactivate it by updating your payment method. Once a successful payment is processed, your account reactivates automatically and you regain full access.

Here's how to reactivate:

1. Go to **[Account & Settings → Payment & Subscription](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account)**.
2. Click **Update card** and enter a valid payment method.
3. Click **Update details** to save. Lusha will immediately attempt to process your outstanding payment.
4. Once the payment succeeds, your account is reactivated and all features are restored.

<Tip>
  If your dashboard is inaccessible due to suspension, you can still reach your billing settings by going directly to [dashboard.lusha.com/account](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account).
</Tip>

If the payment still fails after updating your card, check the error reason above and address that specific issue first. If you're still stuck, contact Lusha Support for help.

If you continue to have trouble, contact Lusha Support using the chat icon in your dashboard or email [support@lusha.com](mailto:support@lusha.com).
