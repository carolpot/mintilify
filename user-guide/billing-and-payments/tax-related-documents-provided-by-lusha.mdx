## How to obtain tax-related documents from Lusha

Lusha provides essential tax-related documents to facilitate international payments and ensure compliance with tax regulations. These documents include the Tax Residency Certificate (TRC), Form 10F, and the No Permanent Establishment (NPE) declaration. Below is a comprehensive guide on how to request these documents and their intended use.

## Overview of tax documentation provided by Lusha

Lusha offers the following tax-related documents upon request:

- **Tax Residency Certificate (TRC):** An official certificate issued by Lusha's tax authority, typically for the current fiscal year or the most recent period. This document is often required for compliance purposes.
- **Form 10F:** A form used for withholding tax purposes, often required by international tax authorities.
- **No Permanent Establishment (NPE) Declaration:** A declaration confirming that Lusha does not have a permanent establishment in a specific country, which may impact tax obligations.

## How to request these documents

These documents are not available through Lusha's standard invoice system. To obtain them, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact Lusha Support">
    Reach out to Lusha's support team via the official support channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Specify your request">
    Clearly state the documents you need (e.g., TRC, Form 10F, NPE declaration) and the purpose for which they are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the documents">
    The support team will provide the requested documents, typically as attachments in an email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Additional notes on compliance and use cases

- These documents are often required for international payment processing and compliance with local tax regulations.
- Ensure you request the documents well in advance to avoid delays in payment processing or compliance reporting.
- If you have specific requirements, such as a TRC for a particular fiscal year, include these details in your request.

By following the steps outlined above, you can easily obtain the necessary tax-related documents from Lusha to meet your compliance and payment processing needs.
