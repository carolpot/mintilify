---

You can view and download your invoices and receipts from your Lusha account at any time. Each invoice is generated at the start of a billing cycle.

<Note>
You must be an Admin to download invoices and receipts.
</Note>

## How to download an invoice or receipt

1. Click your name in the left navigation and go to **Account & Settings**.
2. Select the **[Payment & Subscription](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account/subscription)** tab.
3. Find the payment you want in the table. The most recent payments appear at the top.
4. Click the **three-dot menu (…)** next to the payment and select **View Invoice**.
5. In the new tab, click **Download invoice** or **Download receipt**.

To update your billing details (company name, address, card), see [Update your billing information](/user-guide/billing-and-payments/how-to-update-your-billing-information).

**💡 Note:** Scale users can view invoices from April 2026 onward. For older invoices, please reach out to your Account Manager. Self-Service users have full access to their complete invoice history.

<Note>
Lusha Support can add a VAT number to invoices issued within the last 3 months. Contact Support via the chat icon in your dashboard or email [support@lusha.com](mailto:support@lusha.com).
</Note>
