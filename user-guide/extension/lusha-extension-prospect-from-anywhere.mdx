[The Lusha Extension](/user-guide/extension/getting-started-with-lushas-extension) is a browser add-on for Chrome and Edge that lets you reveal verified contact details — emails, direct dials, and phone numbers - without leaving the page you're on. Instead of switching between tabs or copying data manually, you prospect directly from LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, your CRM, or any company website.

It's the fastest way to get contact data when you're already browsing — one click to reveal, one click to save or export.

## Where it works

- **LinkedIn and Sales Navigator** — reveal contact details from any profile or search results page
- **Your CRM** — enrich contacts inside Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive without leaving the record
- **Any company website** — via **Lusha Everywhere**, the extension works across any B2B site you visit
- **Gmail** — prospect by company from your inbox, filter by department or seniority, and reveal contacts in one place

## What you can do with it

- Reveal verified emails, direct dials, and mobile numbers
- Save contacts to a table with a single click
- Export directly to your connected CRM
- Add contacts to an Engage sequence without switching tabs
- See job change indicators when a contact has recently moved roles

## Plan availability

The Lusha Extension is available on all plans. Some features — such as bulk reveal on Sales Navigator — vary by plan tier.

<Note>
The extension works on Chrome and Edge. Full functionality is not supported on Safari or Firefox.
</Note>

Now you're ready to prospect from wherever you work — no tab switching required.
