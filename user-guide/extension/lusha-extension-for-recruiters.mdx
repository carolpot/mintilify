The Lusha extension works on LinkedIn Recruiter, letting you reveal verified contact details for candidates directly from their profiles and search results — no tab switching required.

<Note>
Recruiter access requires approval. Contact the support team via live chat or email [support@lusha.com](mailto:support@lusha.com) to request it.
</Note>

## How to use the extension on LinkedIn Recruiter

## Candidate people search

Open the Lusha extension, use the search bar to load a table of relevant candidates, then click **Show details** to reveal contact information for the whole table at once.

## Candidate profile swipe

While browsing search results on LinkedIn Recruiter, click the Lusha extension icon to instantly reveal contact details for any candidate that appears in the table.

## Candidate profile pages

Open a candidate's LinkedIn profile and switch to their LinkedIn Recruiter profile. Click the Lusha extension icon to instantly reveal their contact information.

You're all set — the Lusha extension helps you reach the right candidates faster, directly from LinkedIn Recruiter.
