---
title: "Access saved contacts in the Lusha Extension"
description: "How to view and manage your saved contacts from any table directly within the Lusha Extension."
---

The **Saved** tab in the Lusha Extension lets you access and manage your saved contacts without switching between tabs or platforms.

## How to view saved contacts

1. Open the **Lusha Extension** on LinkedIn, your CRM, or any website.
2. Click the **Activity** icon at the bottom.
3. Select the **Saved** tab at the top.
4. Choose the relevant table from the dropdown to display its contacts.

## Manage contacts from the extension

Click the **three dots** next to any contact to send an email, export to your CRM, or move the contact to a different table.

Click a contact's name to view their full profile, including direct dials and emails, LinkedIn and company details, and save options.

<Note>
The Saved tab is available across most websites on the web.
</Note>
