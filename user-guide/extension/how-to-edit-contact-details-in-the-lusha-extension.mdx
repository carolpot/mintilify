---

You can update a contact's phone number, email, or job title directly from the Lusha Extension no need to open the dashboard.

## How to edit contact details

1. Open the **Lusha Extension** on LinkedIn, your CRM, or any website.
2. Select a contact whose details you've already saved or revealed.

3. Hover over the detail field (phone, email, or job title) to reveal the **edit icon**.
