---
title: "Wishlist: saving contacts with no available data"
description: "How to save contacts with no available data to your Lusha tables or CRM using the wishlist feature."
---

Sometimes you browse a LinkedIn profile and Lusha has no contact data for that person yet. Rather than losing track of them, you can save them to your Lusha tables or CRM as a Wishlist contact and update the details later.

## How to save a contact to your wishlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a LinkedIn profile with no data">
    Open the Lusha extension on a LinkedIn profile where no data is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the wishlist button">
    The **wishlist** button will appear automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contact information">
    Add any information you want to include in the editable fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Wishlist contacts are not included when exporting a table to a CSV file.
</Note>

## How to export a wishlist contact to your CRM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    After saving the contact to your wishlist, click **Export**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your CRM and save">
    Select your CRM. Connected integrations appear at the top of the list; other available ones are shown below. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Wishlist contacts can currently be exported to Salesforce, Salesforce Sandbox, Bullhorn, and HubSpot only.
</Note>

You're all set - the contact is saved and ready to be updated whenever their data becomes available in Lusha.
