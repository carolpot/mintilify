---
title: "Do Not Call (DNC) tables"
description: "How to enable Lusha's Do Not Call feature to hide DNC-registered phone numbers from search results and stay compliant."
---

The Do Not Call (DNC) feature hides phone numbers registered on national do-not-call tables from your Lusha search results, extension, tables, and CSV enrichment. This helps you stay compliant with direct marketing regulations in the UK and US.

<Note>
This feature is available on Scale plans only. You must be a Lusha Admin to enable it.
</Note>

<Warning>
Calling a number registered on a DNC table may result in significant fines. Always ensure compliance with applicable regulations before conducting phone outreach.
</Warning>

## How to enable the DNC feature

1. Log in to your [Lusha dashboard](https://dashboard.lusha.com/) and click your name in the left navigation.
2. Go to **Account & Settings → [Privacy](https://dashboard.lusha.com/account/privacy)**.
3. In the **Do Not Call table** section, click the **Select countries to hide** dropdown.
4. Select the countries whose DNC tables you want to apply.

Once enabled, phone numbers on the selected DNC tables will be hidden across Lusha. If a contact has other data points (email, mobile), those will still be visible.
