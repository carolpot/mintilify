---
title: "What are my rights as a data subject?"
description: "An overview of your data subject rights under GDPR and how to exercise them with Lusha."
---

As a data subject, you have the right to understand and control how your personal information is processed. Below is a summary of your rights and how to exercise them with Lusha.

All requests are handled by Lusha's Privacy Team. You may need to complete an identification process before your request can be fulfilled. Contact the Privacy Team at [privacy@lusha.com](mailto:privacy@lusha.com).

## Right of access

You can request access to the personal information Lusha holds about you. Lusha will provide, free of charge: the purposes of processing, categories of data processed, categories of recipients, how long your data is stored, your privacy rights, and information about any data transfers.

## Right of rectification

You can request that Lusha correct, update, or complete any inaccurate or missing data held about you. Lusha may also rectify or remove incomplete or inaccurate information at its own discretion.

## Right of erasure

You can request deletion of your personal information at any time. If there is no overriding legitimate interest to continue processing your data, Lusha will remove it.

## Right to restrict processing

You can request that Lusha restrict processing of your data if you contest its accuracy. Contact the Privacy Team with the relevant details to submit this request.

## Right to data portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. Contact the Privacy Team to make this request.

## Right to object

You have the right to object to the processing of your data. You can use the automated [Removal Form](https://www.lusha.com/privacy-center/request-optout-by-phone/) to have your details permanently deleted from Lusha's system, or contact the Privacy Team at [privacy@lusha.com](mailto:privacy@lusha.com) to request removal directly.

If you believe Lusha is not handling your personal information in accordance with applicable law, you may contact the relevant data protection authority in your region.
