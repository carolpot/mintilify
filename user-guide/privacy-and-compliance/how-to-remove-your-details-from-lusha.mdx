If you've found your contact details in Lusha's database and want them removed, you can do this quickly using our automated removal form.

## How to remove your details from Lusha

1. Go to Lusha's [Removal Form](https://www.lusha.com/privacy-center/request-removal/).
2. Complete and submit the form.

Once submitted, your details will be permanently deleted from Lusha's system.

If you'd prefer we handle the removal for you, contact the Privacy Team directly at [privacy@lusha.com](mailto:privacy@lusha.com).

<Note>
Bulk removal via the form is not currently available. If you need to remove multiple records, contact the Privacy Team directly and they'll be happy to help.
</Note>
