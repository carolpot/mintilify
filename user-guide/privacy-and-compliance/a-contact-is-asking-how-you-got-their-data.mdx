---
title: "A contact is asking how you got their data"
description: "How to respond when a contact asks where you got their details, and how to direct them to Lusha's data removal options."
---

If someone you've contacted asks where you got their information, you can let them know it came from Lusha — a B2B data provider that aggregates professional contact information from public sources.

You're welcome to direct them to the following resources:

- [Lusha's Data page](https://www.lusha.com/our-data/) - explains how Lusha collects and processes data
- [Lusha's GDPR page](https://www.lusha.co/gdpr/) - covers compliance and data subject rights
- [Lusha's Trust Center](https://www.lusha.com/trust-center/) - for any data-related questions or concerns

If they want their details removed from Lusha's database, they can submit a request using the [Lusha Removal Form](https://www.lusha.com/privacy-center/request-removal/). Removal is free and processed automatically.
