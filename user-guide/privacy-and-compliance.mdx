Lusha combines account-level security controls with data protection measures and compliance certifications to help your team use Lusha's data responsibly.

## Account security

- **Password protection** - Lusha enforces minimum password strength requirements, and you can update your password from **Settings**.
- **Single sign-on (SSO)** - Scale plan customers can configure SAML 2.0-based SSO through an identity provider such as Okta or Azure AD, so team members sign in with your company identity provider.
- **Session management** - you can sign out of all active sessions from **Settings**.
- **Inactivity timeout** - Scale plans can configure automatic logout after a set period of inactivity.

## Data security

- **Encryption in transit** - all data transmitted to and from Lusha uses TLS encryption.
- **Encryption at rest** - stored data is encrypted at rest.
- **Access controls** - role-based access control (RBAC) limits what each team member can see and do, based on their assigned role. See [Team management](/user-guide/team-management) for how roles work.

## Compliance

Lusha maintains SOC 2 Type II certification and commits to GDPR compliance. SOC 2 reports are available to Scale plan customers, or to accounts that exceed $10,000 in transactions. Lusha also undergoes penetration testing; its most recent assessment rated findings as very low risk.

<Tip>
  You can find more detail on Lusha's certifications and policies at the [Lusha Trust Center](https://www.lusha.com/trust-center).
</Tip>

## Do Not Call (DNC) tables

Lusha can hide phone numbers that appear on national do-not-call registries across search results, the browser extension, workspace tables, and CSV enrichment. This currently covers the UK (TPS and CTPS) and the US.

<Info>
  DNC tables are available on Scale plans only, and only a Lusha Admin can enable them.
</Info>

To enable DNC filtering, an Admin goes to **Account & Settings → Privacy** and selects which countries' DNC tables to apply. Once enabled, matching phone numbers are hidden, while other contact data - like email - remains visible if available.
