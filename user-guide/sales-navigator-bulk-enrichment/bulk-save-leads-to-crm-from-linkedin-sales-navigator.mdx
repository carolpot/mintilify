---
title: "Bulk save leads to CRM from LinkedIn Sales Navigator"
description: "How to bulk save revealed contacts from LinkedIn Sales Navigator to your CRM using the Lusha extension."
---

When prospecting on LinkedIn Sales Navigator with the Lusha extension, you can reveal multiple contacts and push them directly to your CRM in one action - without exporting each one individually.

## How to bulk save contacts to CRM from Sales Navigator

1. Reveal the contacts you want on LinkedIn Sales Navigator using the Lusha extension.
2. Select the contacts and click **Save selected to**.

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/15392415/f740378b1c70d16b8d884817/Screenshot+2024-06-26+at+5_07_51.png?expires=1780056000&signature=ea52535d7c41243bd2a8ac83e3a2978eb4d1bc8f13eb08a19e768ddecffcbd81&req=0dBszFr%2BrTJk2hj99dpg6sT7KCxmLq7F8XlFXgnCfwEvIdXrSee%2FzGxsG%2BF4%0AVEWZonINO66od8lXIxXYIdxR%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

3. Select your CRM integration. Connected integrations appear at the top of the list under **Connected**; other available ones are shown below under **Not Connected**. Then choose the record type - **Lead** or **Contact**.

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/15392618/d28f8d91ad00fe320a94f72d/Screenshot+2024-06-26+at+5_11_53.png?expires=1780056000&signature=b831c807a22a7cb3e6b92dd02607c5a4273f82e342abedd98b73f115c51db605&req=0dBszFr8rT9k2hj99dpg6vNyh%2B%2FxhUymYQvlq2SxuAUUvEaGH6G%2B2QYJzRPW%0AVkDhaEDNJgT22hnz0NY2AnMC%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

4. If saving as Leads, you can review and edit each contact's company name.

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/15392706/c554985ac95663b9ec7de33e/Screenshot+2024-06-26+at+5_15_36.png?expires=1780056000&signature=a59665fc63b52ef0d9abd3c5e9b547e62f1f5f52c3c5e14704044202d4d44225&req=0dBszFr9rDFk2hj99dpg6uq0J67PTOt2MPOucccz88qM%2BJ4uiGxiKBh5YVQB%0AtPEiiJG4gw1xX92gs%2FzUQM7h%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

5. To assign all contacts to the same account, tick **Save all to the same account**. This locks the other company name fields.

6. Click **Save**.

A loader will appear while Lusha saves the contacts. Once complete, you'll see a confirmation screen. You can then mark it as done or export to another integration.

Contacts saved to your CRM will show the CRM logo next to their name in the extension.

<img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/15392865/f414f980419c425be941f73c/Screenshot+2024-06-26+at+5_18_20.png?expires=1780056000&signature=d444f8280d51ea92a18cb28f837ef9cfc600148598750ea7ab8b14f91be5f93e&req=0dBszFryqjJk2hj99dpg6lQswzEjJrK6hD1nHgvUaxKDqX8YtZ61%2FKOzfr%2BE%0ATPZrFoJtXwv8Pf%2FYRR8N7OXd%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

You're all set - your leads are now in your CRM and ready for follow-up.
