---

Lusha's Bulk Enrichment feature lets you reveal contact details for multiple people at once directly from a LinkedIn Sales Navigator table — without opening each profile individually. It's available through the Lusha Extension.

## Before you start

- Make sure the [Lusha Extension](/user-guide/extension/getting-started-with-lushas-extension) is installed and you're logged in.
- Open LinkedIn Sales Navigator in your browser.
- Navigate to a Lead or Account table.

## How to bulk enrich from Sales Navigator

1. Open a Lead table in LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
2. Click the **Lusha icon** that appears in the top-right corner of the page to open the Lusha panel.
3. Select the contacts you want to enrich — you can choose individually or select all visible on the page.
4. Click **Show details** to reveal their email addresses and phone numbers in bulk.

<Note>
Each reveal uses credits according to your plan. The number of contacts you can bulk reveal at once depends on your plan tier:
</Note>

- Free/Starter: 25
- Pro: 50
- Premium: 150
- Scale: 5,000

## What you can do with the results

After revealing, you can save contacts to a Lusha table, export them to your CRM, or add them directly to a Lusha Engage sequence — all without leaving Sales Navigator.

Now you can enrich entire Sales Navigator tables in a fraction of the time. You're all set!
