---

The **Time zone** filter lets you build a contact table around the clock your prospects actually live in. Instead of guessing which contacts are at their desk, you can filter by a named time zone, by the contact's own local time, or return only contacts who are currently inside working hours.

Until now the closest option was filtering by **Location**, which doesn't line up with time zones - one country can span several zones, and one zone can span several countries. Time zone filters by the thing that actually decides whether someone picks up.

## How to use the Time zone filter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filter panel">
    Open the Workspace filter panel and click **Filter contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the CONTACTS group">
    In the filter panel, open the **CONTACTS** group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Time zone">
    Select **Time zone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how you want to filter">
    - **Timezone** - pick a location, for example **Chicago, Illinois**
    - **Local time** - filter by the contact's own clock instead of a named zone
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow to working hours">
    To return only contacts who are at their desk right now, tick **Working Hours Only** and set a **Start** and **End** range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Results refresh in the table on the right as you apply the filter. Time zone stacks with **Job title**, **Seniority**, **Location** and the rest of your filters, and you can clear it at any time.

## Key concepts

- **Timezone** - a named zone selected by location. Use this when you're building a call block for a specific region.
- **Local time** - the contact's own clock. Use this when you care about the hour a contact is in, regardless of which named zone produces it.
- **Working Hours Only** - a toggle that returns only contacts whose current local time falls inside the **Start** and **End** range you set. Lusha does the time-zone math for you, so you don't have to calculate it row by row.

<Note>
  The **Time zone** filter is currently available in the Workspace search filter panel only. It is not yet available in the Lusha API or the Lusha MCP server.
</Note>

## How it fits your workflow

Cold calling depends on local time. A rep working a morning block needs contacts who are also in their morning - not people about to sit down to dinner. Use **Time zone** to:

- Build a table for the hours you're actually dialing, so a call block matches your day
- Prioritize contacts who are reachable now with **Working Hours Only**
- Target a region precisely, without relying on country or city as a stand-in for the clock
