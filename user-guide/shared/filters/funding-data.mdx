title: "Funding data"
description: "How to use Lusha's Funding filter to find companies that have recently raised capital, filtered by amount, stage, type, and funding round."
---

The Funding filter lets you identify ICP companies that have recently raised capital, so you can time your outreach to accounts with fresh budget and growth momentum.

<Note>
Funding data is available across the Lusha platform: [Search](/user-guide/getting-started-with-prospecting/getting-started-with-lusha-prospecting), [Company page](/user-guide/getting-started-with-prospecting/company-360-company-page), and [the Extension](/user-guide/extension/getting-started-with-lushas-extension).
</Note>

## How to use the Funding filter

Go to the [Prospect search](https://dashboard.lusha.com/prospecting/contacts) tab and click on the **Funding** filter on the left side of the screen.

First, select the **time frame** — you can filter by the last round or any round.

You can also select the amount range you'd like to see.

## Funding data in the Lusha extension

Funding information is displayed in the extension under the **Company info** section. To filter by specific funding criteria, use the Search tool as described above.

With the Funding filter, you can focus your prospecting on the accounts most likely to have the budget and momentum to act.
