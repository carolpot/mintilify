---
title: "Access your complete contacts table"
description: "How to find and view every contact you've ever revealed in Lusha using the All shown contacts view."
---

Lusha stores every contact you've ever revealed in one place - the **All shown contacts** view. If you're looking for a contact you revealed previously but can't find it in a specific table, this is where to look.

## How to open All shown contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open All tables">
    In the left sidebar, click **All tables**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click All shown contacts">
    At the top right of the page, click the purple **All shown contacts** button. This opens a unified view of every contact revealed in your account, across all tables.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/user-guide/shared-tables/all-shown-contacts.png" alt="All tables page with the purple All shown contacts button at the top right" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  The **All shown contacts** view is read-only - it reflects your full reveal history. To work with a subset of these contacts, add them to a table.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **You cannot export the All shown contacts table.** To export contacts, save them to another table first, then export from there. See [Exporting from a workspace table](/user-guide/managing-and-exporting-data/exporting-from-a-workspace-table).
</Warning>

## What you'll see

The view shows all revealed contacts with their name, title, company, and contact data. You can sort and search within the table to find a specific person.
