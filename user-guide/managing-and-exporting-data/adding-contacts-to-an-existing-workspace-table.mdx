---
title: "Adding Contacts to an Existing Table"
description: "How to grow a saved table by adding AI-suggested lookalike contacts or using the chat and filter panel to find more matches."
---

After you save a table, you're not locked into the original results. You can grow your table at any time by adding new contacts manually through filters or by letting Lusha's AI suggest lookalike prospects.

## How to add contacts

Open any saved table. At the bottom-left of the table, click **Add contacts**. A menu appears with two options:

**1. Add similar contacts (AI-powered)**

**2. Add from search / filters** *(applies your existing criteria to find more matches)*

## Option 1: Add similar contacts (AI recommendations)

This is Lusha's AI lookalike feature. When you select **Add similar contacts**, Workspace analyzes the contacts already in your table — looking at patterns like industry, seniority level, company size, geography, and department — and surfaces 5 new prospects that closely match your existing table profile.

**How it works:**

- Lusha scans your table daily and generates a fresh batch of 5 suggestions based on your saved contacts
- The suggestions appear as a preview you can review before accepting
- If you don't like the suggestions, click **Regenerate** to get a new set of 5
- Once you're happy, click to add them to your table
- Web data enrichment and signals become available after you save

**When to use it:** This feature is best when you have an established ICP already reflected in your table and you want to continuously grow it with similar-quality prospects without re-doing your search from scratch.

## Option 2: Build from chat or filters

You can also use the AI chat panel on the left side of any saved table to describe additional contacts you want to add. Type a follow-up message like:

- "Add 50 more contacts from the healthcare industry"
- "Find me contacts similar to the ones already in this table but in Canada"

The AI will update the table with additional matching results.

## Save options and plan limits

When adding contacts from the search and filter panel, you can select contacts manually or use the header checkbox dropdown — options include selecting all on page, a custom amount, or all contacts matching your active filters. This matches the bulk selection behavior in Prospecting, and is not capped by page size. The preset **Save selected** amounts remain available: **50, 150, 500,** or **1,000 contacts**.

The same improved bulk selection is available in the **Add Contacts** and **Add Companies** modals.

How many contacts you can view at once depends on your plan:

- **From workspace search** (previewing results before saving): up to 150 contacts at once on Premium and Scale; up to 25 on Free and Pro
- **In a saved table:** up to 1,000 contacts per page on Premium and Scale; up to 25 on Free and Pro

<Note>
To save more contacts than your current preview limit allows, use the preset save amounts — for example, choose **Save 500** or **Save 1000** from the **Save selected** dropdown.
</Note>

## AI recommendations on the homepage

In addition to the "Add contacts" button inside tables, Workspace also surfaces recommendations on your home screen under "Recommended leads tailored just for you." These are auto-generated daily based on your overall prospecting activity across all tables.

To use them: Click **View all** on any recommendation card. This opens a temporary table with up to 75 contacts or companies.

You can explore, add enrichment columns, and sort or filter the results before saving.

Click **"Save first X contacts"** (options: 25, 50, or 75) to convert the temporary table into a permanent one. The saved table will be named "[Recommendation name] — [Date]" automatically.

## Tips

- The more contacts you save across your tables, the smarter the AI recommendations become — the AI learns from your prospecting patterns over time
- "Add similar contacts" works best on tables with at least 25–50 saved contacts for the AI to detect meaningful patterns
- You can run "Add similar contacts" multiple times on the same table on different days to keep a table growing over time
