---
title: "Managing first and last name fields in exports"
description: "Lusha exports full names as a single field. Here's what that means and how to split them after export."
---

When you export contacts from Lusha, the contact's full name is exported as a single field. Lusha does not split first and last names into separate columns automatically, and there is no option to customise which fields are included in the export.

If you need separate first and last name columns, you can split them manually in Excel or Google Sheets after the export. This article explains how.

## What Lusha includes in the export

Every export from Lusha includes all available contact fields. The full name appears as a single column - there is no way to separate it before or during the export, regardless of whether contacts have been revealed or saved to a table.

## How to split names in Microsoft Excel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the export in Excel">
    Export your contacts from Lusha as usual and open the file in **Microsoft Excel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the full name column">
    Click the column header containing the full name to select it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Text to Columns">
    Go to **Data → Text to Columns**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delimited">
    Select **Delimited** and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Space as the delimiter">
    Check the **Space** box as your delimiter and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the destination and finish">
    Choose a destination column for the split data and click **Finish**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Label and review the columns">
    Label the new columns as needed (e.g. **First Name**, **Last Name**) and review the results for any names that didn't split as expected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Names with prefixes, suffixes, or multiple words (e.g. "Dr. Jane van Der Berg") may not split cleanly using a space delimiter. Review those rows manually after splitting.
</Note>

## How to split names in Google Sheets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the export in Google Sheets">
    Open your exported file in **Google Sheets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the SPLIT formula">
    In an empty column next to the full name column, enter the formula:\
    ​`=SPLIT(A2," ")`\
    Replace `A2` with the actual cell reference for your first name entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the formula">
    Press **Enter**. The formula splits the name at each space and populates the adjacent columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill down to all rows">
    Drag the formula down to apply it to all rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Label and review the columns">
    Label the resulting columns (e.g. **First Name**, **Last Name**) and review the results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you've split the name fields, you're ready to use your contact data however you need.
