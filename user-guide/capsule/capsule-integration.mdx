---
title: "Capsule integration"
description: "How to connect Lusha to Capsule, configure field mapping, and push enriched contact and organization data to your Capsule workspace."
---

The Lusha–Capsule integration lets you push enriched contact and organization data from Lusha directly into Capsule, removing the need to copy and paste between platforms. Once connected, any contact you reveal in Lusha can be exported to Capsule with data mapped to the fields you configure.

<Note>
  Editing integration settings requires Admin or Manager access in Lusha. You must also be a Capsule admin to activate the integration.
</Note>

---

## How to connect Capsule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your name menu">
    Log in to your [Lusha dashboard](https://dashboard.lusha.com/) and click your name in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Capsule">
    Go to the [Integrations tab](https://dashboard.lusha.com/lusha-integrations) and select **Capsule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** - you'll be redirected to the Capsule login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and authorize">
    Sign in to Capsule if prompted and authorize Lusha's access to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to field mapping">
    Once authorized, click **Continue to field mapping**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the record types">
    In the Field mapping environment, you'll see two record types: **People** and **Organisations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Allow Override if needed">
    To update existing records in your database, toggle on **Allow Override**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Allow Override is a Lusha setting that controls whether Lusha will update fields that already have a value in Capsule. When toggled off, Lusha will only populate empty fields.
</Note>

---

## Adding predefined value fields

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the record type">
    Choose the record type (**People** or **Organisations**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a predefined value field">
    Click **+ Add field** and select **Predefined Value**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Capsule field">
    Select a Capsule field from the available options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the predefined value">
    Enter the predefined value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save changes** before switching tabs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Account export preferences

Admins can control which object types users can export to Capsule. When the toggle is off, users can only save contacts to existing Capsule accounts - not create new ones.

<Note>
  These settings apply to all team members in your account.
</Note>

To manage export preferences, go to **Integrations → Capsule → Settings tab**.

---

## Default field mapping

If you modify your field mapping and need to revert to the original settings, use the tables below as a reference.

### People

| Lusha Field | Capsule Field |
| --- | --- |
| Full Name   | First Name + Last Name |
| Emails      | Email                  |
| Phones      | Phone                  |
| Job Title   | Job Title              |

### Organisations

| Lusha Field     | Capsule Field |
| --- | --- |
| Company Name    | Name          |
| Company Street  | Address       |
| Company Website | Website       |

To learn more about field mapping values, see [Fields for field mapping and descriptions](/user-guide/integrations-basics/fields-for-field-mapping-and-descriptions).
