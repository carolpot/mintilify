---
title: "How to export contacts to CRM using Lusha"
description: "How to export contacts from Lusha to your CRM from Lusha Search, the extension, or your contact tables."
---

You can push contacts revealed in Lusha directly to your CRM from three places: Lusha Search, the Lusha extension, and your Contact tables.

Export limits vary by CRM - up to 150 contacts to Salesforce, 100 to HubSpot, and 25 to all other supported CRMs.

<Note>
Each CRM export costs 1 credit, charged at the time of export and separate from the original reveal cost.
</Note>

<Note>
HubSpot now also supports pushing companies directly from Lusha. When exporting from Lusha's company search, select **HubSpot** and choose **Company** as the record type. You can control company record creation in **Settings → Integrations → HubSpot → Settings tab**. See [HubSpot integration](/user-guide/hubspot/hubspot-integration) for full details.
</Note>

## From Lusha Search (Prospecting)

1. Go to [Lusha Search](https://dashboard.lusha.com/prospecting/contacts) and find the contacts you want.
2. Select the contacts and click **Show details**.
3. Click the **CRM** button in the action bar.
4. Select the CRM integration you want to export to. If you have connected integrations, they appear at the top of the list under **Connected**. Other available integrations are shown below under **Not Connected**.
5. Choose the record type (Lead, Contact, Company, etc.).

Lusha will detect the relevant account or company name automatically. If no existing record is found, it will flag that a new account will be created (shown in green).

6. To assign all selected contacts to the same account, click **Assign all [#] contacts to an account**, then select or type the account name.

7. Click **Save**. A confirmation screen will appear when the export is complete.

## From the Lusha extension

1. Open the extension and reveal the contact's details.
2. Click **CRM**.
3. Select your CRM and the record type. Connected integrations appear at the top of the list; other available ones are shown below.

## From Contact tables

1. Open a table from the left navigation panel.
2. Select the number of contacts you would like to export.
3. Click **CRM** and follow the same steps as above.

Please note that each integration has export limits.

Up to 150 contacts to Salesforce, 100 to HubSpot, and 25 to all other supported CRMs.

## Troubleshooting

- **Mapping errors** - check your field mapping configuration in Integrations settings.
- **Integration issues** - go to Integrations and reauthenticate your CRM connection.
