---
title: "How to export contacts to CRM using Lusha"
description: "How to export contacts from Lusha to your CRM from Lusha Search, the extension, or your contact tables."
---

You can push contacts revealed in Lusha directly to your CRM from three places: Lusha Search, the Lusha extension, and your tables.

Exports within the real-time threshold complete instantly. Larger selections are processed in the background - up to 10,000 records for Salesforce, and 1,000 for other CRMs. These limits are set by each CRM platform and cannot be changed. Async export beyond the real-time threshold is available for contacts export only - company exports are limited to the real-time threshold.

### Per-CRM export limits

| CRM | Real-Time Limit | Max Per Async Export | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Salesforce | Up to 150 | 10,000 | Sandbox included, [Identifier](/user-guide/salesforce/salesforce-identifier-indicator) available |
| HubSpot | Up to 100 | 1,000 | [Identifier](/user-guide/hubspot/hubspot-identifier-indicator) available |
| Bullhorn | Up to 25 | 1,000 | [Identifier](/user-guide/bullhorn/bullhorn-identifier-indicator) available |
| Pipedrive | Up to 25 | 1,000 | |
| Zoho | Up to 50 | 1,000 | |
| Outreach | Up to 25 | - | Save to sequence available |
| SalesLoft | Up to 25 | - | Save to cadence available |
| MS Dynamics | Up to 25 | 1,000 | |
| [Capsule](/user-guide/capsule/capsule-integration) | Up to 25 | - | Pushes enriched contacts and organizations, field mapping configurable |

For the full breakdown, see [Available CRM integrations](/user-guide/integrations-basics/available-crm-integrations).

<Note>
Each CRM export costs 1 credit, charged at the time of export and separate from the original reveal cost.
</Note>

<Note>
HubSpot now also supports pushing companies directly from Lusha. When exporting from Lusha's company search, select **HubSpot** and choose **Company** as the record type. You can control company record creation in **Settings → Integrations → HubSpot → Settings tab**. See [HubSpot integration](/user-guide/hubspot/hubspot-integration) for full details.
</Note>

## From Lusha Search (Prospecting)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your contacts in Lusha Search">
    Go to [Lusha Search](https://dashboard.lusha.com/prospecting/contacts) and find the contacts you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select contacts and show details">
    Select the contacts and click **Show details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the CRM button">
    Click the **CRM** button in the action bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the CRM integration">
    Select the CRM integration you want to export to. If you have connected integrations, they appear at the top of the list under **Connected**. Other available integrations are shown below under **Not Connected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the record type">
    Choose the record type (Lead, Contact, Company, etc.).

    Lusha will detect the relevant account or company name automatically. If no existing record is found, it will flag that a new account will be created (shown in green).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign contacts to an account (optional)">
    To assign all selected contacts to the same account, click **Assign all [#] contacts to an account**, then select or type the account name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the export">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation screen will appear when the export is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## From the Lusha extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reveal the contact's details">
    Open the extension and reveal the contact's details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click CRM">
    Click **CRM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the CRM and record type">
    Select your CRM and the record type. Connected integrations appear at the top of the list; other available ones are shown below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export from a table

Open a table, select the rows to export (or leave none checked to target the whole view), then click the **CRM** button in the toolbar to open the export drawer. From there, choose the CRM and record type (**Export as**). Then set your export preferences: whether to skip already-exported contacts, how to handle contacts with unshown details, and whether to update contacts already in your CRM.

See [Exporting from a workspace table](/user-guide/managing-and-exporting-data/exporting-from-a-workspace-table#export-option-2-crm-sync) for the full walkthrough.

## When your export runs in the background

Large table exports run asynchronously. While the job runs:

- A floating progress window shows batch-by-batch progress. Closing or minimizing it does not cancel the job.
- You can keep working in Lusha.
- When the export is complete, a banner appears in the table and you'll receive an in-app notification. You can see the full details in the export list, and check the status on the integration page.

<Note>
Only one export can run at a time per user. The CRM button is disabled while a job is in flight - hover over it to see the status.
</Note>

### Export state on your table

Each row displays a status pill - **Exported** or **Not exported** - showing whether a record was pushed in a completed job. After a successful export, rows flip to **Exported**. If a contact fails or the export is truncated (scope exceeds the CRM cap), it stays **Not exported** and will be picked up automatically on your next run.

<Warning>
Export state reflects whether a record was sent from Lusha. It does not reflect the record's status inside your CRM - such as any edits, deletions, or status changes made after the export.
</Warning>

## Reviewing your CRM exports

To see a history of all exports, go to **Settings → Integrations → the relevant integration → Manage → Integration tab**.

The CRM export tab lists every table pushed to a connected CRM in the last 90 days, with these columns:

| Column | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| CRM | The destination platform |
| Source table | The Lusha table the export came from |
| Time | Date and time the job ran |
| Status | Done / In progress / Completed with errors / Failed |
| Exported / total | How many records were successfully pushed |
| Failed | How many records failed (shown in red when non-zero) |

Select any job to see which contacts failed and why.

<Note>
The CRM export tab shows export-level results from Lusha. To see exactly which contacts are now in your CRM, go to your CRM directly and filter records by source - look for contacts where the source is set to Lusha.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

- **Mapping errors** - check your field mapping in **Settings → Integrations → the relevant integration → Manage → Integration tab**.
- **Integration issues** - go to **Settings → Integrations → the relevant integration → Manage → Integration tab** and reauthenticate your CRM connection. A disconnected token will cause a full job failure, shown as **Failed** in the CRM export tab.
- **Contacts not appearing in CRM after export** - check the CRM export tab under Account to confirm the job status. If the job shows **Completed with errors**, select it to see how many contacts failed.

You're all set - your contacts are on their way to your CRM.
