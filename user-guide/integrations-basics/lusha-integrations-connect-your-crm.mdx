Lusha integrates natively with the CRMs your team already uses. Once connected, you can export contacts directly from Lusha into your CRM, enrich existing records with verified data, and keep your pipeline accurate - without manual data entry or CSV imports.

Integrations are managed by Admins and Managers, and apply across your whole account once set up.

## Supported CRMs

- **Salesforce** - export contacts and companies, enrich existing leads and accounts, and sync Conversations recordings as Event activities
- **Salesforce Sandbox** - connect to your sandbox environment to test your saved lead and contact data before pushing to production
- **HubSpot** - push targeted tables and capture leads directly into HubSpot
- **Pipedrive** - bulk save prospect tables and turn opportunities into booked meetings
- **Zoho CRM** - save relevant leads to your Zoho database and convert them into opportunities
- **Bullhorn** - export leads, contacts, and candidates into the right applicant tracking process
- **Dynamics CRM** - instantly save the right lead and contact data to the right Dynamics CRM field

## What you can do with integrations

- Export contacts from Workspace, Search, tables, or the Extension directly into your CRM
- Map Lusha data fields to your CRM fields so data lands in the right place
- Enable **Allow Override** to update existing records with fresher data
- Use **CRM Enrichment workflows** to bulk-enrich Salesforce leads, contacts, and accounts on a schedule (legacy Scale plans)
- Filter out contacts already in your CRM using the **Not in my CRM** filter in Workspace

## How to connect your CRM

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and select your CRM. You'll need Admin or Manager permissions to connect and configure an integration.

<Note>
Export limits vary by CRM - a single export supports up to **10,000 contacts to Salesforce** and up to **1,000 to HubSpot and all other CRMs**. Each CRM also has a lower real-time threshold; exports above it run in the background. See [Available CRM integrations](/user-guide/integrations-basics/available-crm-integrations) for the full per-CRM breakdown.
</Note>

Now you're ready to keep your CRM clean, current, and connected to Lusha.
