---
title: "Gmail integration setup"
description: "Connect your Google account to use Lusha Engage email sequences and Conversations calendar sync."
---

The Gmail integration connects your Google account to Lusha. Once connected, you can send emails from your Lusha Engage sequences using your own Gmail address, and Lusha Conversations can sync with your Google Calendar to automatically record scheduled meetings.

<Note>
Connecting Gmail serves two purposes at once - email sending via Engage sequences, and calendar sync for Conversations auto-recording. You only need to connect once.
</Note>

## Before you start

- You need a Google Workspace or personal Gmail account.
- You need at least User-level permissions in your Lusha account.

## How to connect Gmail

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations">
    Go to **Integrations** - click your name at the bottom-left of the sidebar, then select **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Gmail card">
    Under the **Email Service** category, click the **Gmail** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Google">
    A Google authorization window will open. Sign in to the Google account you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow the requested permissions">
    Review the permissions Lusha is requesting and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    You'll return to the Integrations page and see Gmail listed as **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your Gmail address is now available as a sender in **Engagement → Engage → Sequences**, and calendar sync is active for **Engagement → Conversations**.

<Note>
You can also connect Gmail directly from within Engage settings. Both paths create the same connection.
</Note>

<Warning>
Connecting Gmail grants Lusha permission to send emails on your behalf and to read your calendar events. You can disconnect at any time from the Integrations page by clicking the Gmail card and selecting **Disconnect**.
</Warning>

You're all set! Your Gmail account is connected for both email sequences and meeting recording.
