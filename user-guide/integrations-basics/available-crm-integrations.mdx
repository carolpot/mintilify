## Lusha Partner Integrations

In addition to native CRM integrations, Lusha partners with third-party platforms that connect to Lusha via API. These are not direct CRM push integrations - they embed Lusha data into their own workflows (enrichment pipelines, AI agents, outreach sequences, and more). Each partner manages the connection on their side.

| Partner | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scalestack | GTM orchestration | Enriches, scores, and routes records across your CRM and engagement tools using AI agents |
| SourceWhale | Outreach & recruitment automation | Pulls verified contact data into multi-channel sequences and syncs activity back to your CRM |
| Chili Piper | Form enrichment & scheduling | Enriches inbound form submissions with contact and company data using just an email address |
| Monday.com | Work management | Automatically enriches contact and company records in your Monday.com boards |
| Wyzard.ai | Revenue intelligence | Triggers real-time contact enrichment based on prospect behavior, with automatic CRM sync |
| Webmetic | Website visitor identification | Enriches anonymous B2B website visitors with verified decision-maker contact details |
| Augmented 4 | AI agent platform | Feeds verified contact data into AI agents for automated lead qualification and outreach |
