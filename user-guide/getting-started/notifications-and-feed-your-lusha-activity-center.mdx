## The Notifications panel

The Notifications panel is the bell icon in the top-left area of every Lusha page. You can also open it with the keyboard shortcut **Alt+T**.

When there are new updates, the bell shows a red dot indicator. Click it to open the panel and review your notifications.

## What appears in Notifications

The Notifications panel surfaces system-level alerts and important account updates relevant to you. These include things like:

- Credit balance warnings (approaching or reaching your limit)
- Workspace or table updates that require your attention
- Product announcements and feature updates from Lusha
- Admin actions that affect your account (e.g. role or credit limit changes, join account invitations)

When there are no new updates, the panel shows "No new updates."

<Note>
Notifications are personal — each user sees alerts relevant to their own account activity and role. Admins may see additional account-level alerts that regular users do not.
</Note>

## The Feed

The Feed is a separate real-time activity stream, accessible via the Feed button on your dashboard home screen. It is distinct from the Notifications panel.

The Feed surfaces **signal-based updates about the companies in your tables** — essentially a live stream of what's happening across your target accounts. When companies in your saved tables or Workspace tables show significant buying signals or changes, those updates appear here.

To open the Feed, click the Feed icon on your dashboard home screen. Inside, you'll see:

- A chronological table of updates about companies you're tracking
- A **Refresh Feed** button to pull in the latest activity
- A **Close Feed** button to dismiss the panel

When there is no recent activity matching your saved tables or signals configuration, the Feed shows: "We have no updates for you at this time. Please check again later."

**Feed vs. Notifications — what's the difference?**

|  | Notifications | Feed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What it shows** | System alerts, account events, product updates | Signal-based company activity from your tables |
| **How to open** | Bell icon (top left) or Alt+T | Feed button on home screen |
| **Triggered by** | Platform events and admin actions | Changes in companies you're tracking |
| **Frequency** | As events happen | Refreshed on demand |

## The home screen AI Recommendations

In addition to Notifications and Feed, your Lusha home screen shows a unified **AI Recommendations feed** powered by ICP Hub. This replaced the previous separate signal widgets (activity-based, intent-based, and CRM-based).

Every morning, up to 10,000 contacts and 10,000 companies are ranked and ready on your Home page across two scored tables. Each lead shows:

- Its **score** — a combination of ICP fit and signal strength
- The **Signals** behind it — new role, promotion, intent, hiring growth, CRM match, and more
- Which **Target Profile** it matched — so you know exactly why it surfaced

The feed is built from four dimensions of context: your ICP Hub configuration, your search and reveal history, your connected CRM data, and live signals. The more precisely you've configured ICP Hub, the more targeted your results.

<Note>
If you haven't set up ICP Hub yet, your home screen will show a "Lusha generated" feed based on your account history. Create a Target Profile in ICP Hub to get a fully scored, ICP-matched feed. See How to Create a Target Profile to get started.
</Note>

## Tips for getting the most from Notifications and Feed

- Set up your **ICP Hub** with Target Profiles to ensure your home screen recommendations reflect your actual ICP. The more precisely you define your criteria, the more actionable the results.
- Connect your **CRM** via [Integrations](/user-guide/integrations-basics/crm-permissions-required-for-lusha-integration) to feed CRM data into your AI recommendations scoring and set up CRM-based exclusion rules.
- Build and save **[Workspace Tables](/user-guide/introduction-and-getting-started/workspace-ai-powered-table-building)** with your target accounts so Lusha has the right companies to track signals for in your Feed.
- Check your **Notifications** before the start of your week to catch any credit or account alerts that need attention.
