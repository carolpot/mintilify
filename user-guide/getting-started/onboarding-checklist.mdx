New accounts see a **Let's get started** checklist on the Lusha dashboard, opened from a rocket icon. It walks you through a short set of setup steps to help you get value from Lusha quickly.

<Note>
This is a brief overview - the individual checklist steps aren't documented here yet.
</Note>

## Where to find it

Look for the rocket icon on your dashboard. Clicking it opens the **Let's get started** checklist, which takes about 8 minutes to complete in full.

See [Your Lusha dashboard - a quick tour](/user-guide/getting-started/your-lusha-dashboard-a-quick-tour) for an overview of the rest of the dashboard.
