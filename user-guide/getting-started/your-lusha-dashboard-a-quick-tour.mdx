When you first log in to Lusha, you land on the Dashboard. This article is a 4-minute orientation to help you understand what's on your screen and where everything lives.

## **Getting to the Dashboard**

Go to [dashboard.lusha.com](https://dashboard.lusha.com) and log in with your Lusha credentials. If you're using SSO, click Sign in with SSO and enter your company email.

## **The main areas of the Dashboard**

## Left navigation panel

This is your main menu, always visible on the left side of the screen. It gives you access to every Lusha product and feature:

- **+ New table** - start a new workspace table
- **Home** - your dashboard overview and recent activity
- **Prospecting** - build and manage detailed prospect lists

**Tables**
- **All tables** - your saved contact and company tables (previously "Lists"), with sub-sections:
  - **Recents** - recently accessed tables
  - **AI Recommendations** - AI-suggested prospects based on your ICP
  - **Website visitors** (Beta) - contacts identified from your website traffic

**Tools**
- **API & connectors** - access the API hub and integration connectors
- **MCP** - connect Claude, ChatGPT, n8n, and other AI tools
- **Engagement** - email sequences and outreach
- **CSV enrichment** - enrich large lists of contacts with Lusha data
- **Resources** - help and documentation

## **Account menu**

Click your name at the bottom-left of the sidebar to open the account menu. From here you can access:
- **Settings** - profile, billing, permissions, and more
- **Invite member** - add team members to your account
- **Team management** - manage roles and credit limits (Admin/Manager only)
- **View plans** - see available Lusha plans
- **Integrations** - connect your CRM and other tools
- **Analytics** - usage reports and insights
- **API documentation** - technical reference for developers
- **Help & support** - access the Help Center and Campus
- **Log out**

## **Home screen widgets**

The Dashboard home screen shows an overview of your recent activity and quick-access widgets:

- Quick search - a shortcut to launch a Workspace or Search query without navigating away
- Recents - fast access to your recent workspace activity
- AI Recommendations preview - a snapshot of your latest recommended prospects

## **Where to go next?**

Now that you know your way around, here's what most users do first:

- [Create a new Workspace](/user-guide/introduction-and-getting-started/workspace-ai-powered-table-building) - Build tables using AI, enrich them with real-time web signals, and sync them directly to your sales stack- all without leaving the page
- [Start prospecting](/user-guide/introduction-and-getting-started/workspace-ai-powered-table-building) - drop a quick search and start finding your ICPs in your workspace
- Install the Extension - add the Lusha Chrome Extension to prospect directly from LinkedIn and your CRM
- [Connect your CRM](https://info.lusha.com/en/collections/314220-integrations) - click your name at the bottom-left of the sidebar, select Integrations, and link Salesforce, HubSpot, or another supported CRM
- Discover our [Lusha Plays](https://www.lusha.com/campus/plays/) - Ready-to-run GTM workflows built on Lusha's verified data and signals for cleaner CRMs and active pipelines.
- Check your credits - your credit balance is shown at the bottom of the sidebar. Click your name to see a full breakdown.
