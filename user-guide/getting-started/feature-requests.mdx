We've added a new way for you to share feedback and request features. You can now submit ideas directly through our [feedback portal](https://ideas.lusha.com) — see what others are asking for, vote on the ones you care about, and get updates when your request moves forward.

## How it works

You can access the feature request portal from two places:

## From the platform

1. Click on your name in the left navigation.
2. Select **Submit feature request**.

2. In the **Lusha Platform** section, click **Submit feature request**.

## How to submit your idea

Once you get to the feedback portal:

1. Start typing your idea in the search bar.
2. If a similar idea already exists, you can vote for it.

   <img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/yu27vhse/54816081/1d6ae957eea797c93aa2885c415e/AD_4nXfPZz9u6LhlOUZg1v72LqqZknxrEAW4C6R6Ap-q_RMB0gT3_rU91y4omDXFtEwxrIIM_eXieWEhPYCJt5UaDpXttodc65kqfM9XpeoNoYPhvCs9ofsSOJc2cAS-DXfCLtttWbLUJw?expires=1780056000&signature=407c160ce720a04f04415c4986059e0ec708d2b1603fcda0ada506de9d09836c&req=1dFnxF76pDZk2hj99dpg6h4Y30uNTwQ1qFvvinIfDtsjKTa0VnoFHNXCzuEw%0AZNU1GElJo%2Br5PzIaMUhCJ29A%0A" alt="" style={{maxWidth: "100%", height: "auto"}} />

3. To submit something new, click **Post a new idea**.
4. Fill in a clear, descriptive title.
5. Select the most relevant category (e.g. Engage, tables & Automation, API).
6. Add an optional longer description with details or use cases.
7. Click **Post idea**.

Once submitted, your idea will be reviewed by a moderator and you'll receive an email confirmation.

## How to support an existing idea

You can support other users' ideas by voting to show it matters to you, or leaving a comment to add more context, use cases, or examples. Supporting existing ideas helps our team identify common needs across users.

## How to change your profile information

If you'd like your posts to be anonymous, you can change your display name in settings.
