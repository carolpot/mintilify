**Lusha Talent Sourcing** is a recruiting-focused plugin that connects Lusha's candidate data to your AI assistant. It runs on its own MCP server, separate from the general [Lusha MCP](/mcp/overview), with tools and skills built specifically for sourcing and tracking candidates rather than sales prospecting.

<Note>
  Lusha Talent Sourcing is a distinct product from the general Lusha MCP server described elsewhere in these docs. It uses its own connection and its own set of tools, described below. See [Assumptions to verify](#assumptions-to-verify) before you publish or share setup steps with customers.
</Note>

## What you can do with it

- Ask for candidates who fit a role and are likely open to a move right now, with a reason to approach each one
- Come back to a saved candidate list later and ask what's changed - who moved, who was promoted, or whose employer had news worth acting on
- Ask who's likely in play after a leadership change at a company you're hiring from
- Search by role, seniority, department, location, current employer, previous employer, and certifications
- Save candidates to a list without revealing any contact details, then reveal details only for the candidates you choose

## How it's different from Lusha MCP

| | Lusha MCP | Lusha Talent Sourcing |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Sales and GTM teams | Recruiters and talent acquisition teams |
| Tool names | Sales-oriented (for example, `prospecting_contact_search`) | Recruiting-oriented (for example, `talent_search`) |
| Skills | General enrichment and prospecting | Source movable talent, keep a list live, leadership change sourcing |
| Contact reveal | Default behavior varies by tool | Off by default - a search returns candidates first; you choose who to reveal |

If your workflow is sales prospecting rather than candidate sourcing, use [Lusha MCP](/mcp/overview) instead.

## Setup

1. Find **Lusha Talent Sourcing** in your AI assistant's plugin or connector directory
2. Add or connect the plugin and complete the authentication flow
3. Confirm the Lusha Talent Sourcing tools appear in your conversation's tools menu

<Note>
  You need an active Lusha account to reveal enriched candidate data. Search and list-management tools work without revealing contact details.
</Note>

Tool calls draw from the same Lusha credit pool as your other Lusha usage - see your [API keys page](https://dashboard.lusha.com/api/manage-api-keys) to check your account.

## Assumptions to verify

This article was drafted from an internal scope document and a marketplace submission record, not a finalized public spec. Confirm the following before publishing:

- **Which assistants it's actually available on.** The marketplace submission record describes a Claude plugin directory listing. A ChatGPT plugin link was also shared for this capability, but it wasn't reachable to verify, and nothing in the source material confirms a ChatGPT release. Confirm the actual client(s) before this article names any beyond Claude.
- **Exact setup steps and directory listing name.** The steps above are written generically ("find it in your plugin directory") pending confirmation of the exact install flow end users will see.
- **Tool and skill names.** Recruiter-facing tool names (for example, `talent_search`) come from an internal naming crosswalk that may still change before general release.
- **Scope of contact search filters.** Some filters recruiters might expect - screening by skill, education, or language - are known limitations in the source material and shouldn't be implied as available until confirmed for the shipped version.
- **Pricing and credit behavior.** This article intentionally avoids stating specific credit costs per action, in line with our documentation style guide. Confirm with Product whether any recruiting-specific pricing exists before this goes live, and if a pricing note belongs on this page.
- **Geographic or account restrictions.** The source material notes at least one region without candidate coverage. Confirm whether this needs a caveat here.
