<Accordion title="Where does Lusha's data come from?">
  Lusha aggregates data from publicly available sources and contributions from trusted business partners. Data is not created or directly managed by Lusha. For full details, see the [Privacy Policy](https://lusha.com/legal/privacy-notice/).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How much data does Lusha cover?">
  Lusha's data catalog spans roughly 290 million contacts and 29 million companies, across around 98 verified contact and company fields - split between "Data Points" (static verified attributes, such as job title, industry, or revenue) and "Signals" (real-time events, such as hiring surges, role changes, or funding news).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How fresh is the data?">
  Most Data Points refresh on a regular cadence (commonly weekly). Signal-driven fields - like a contact's current job - update as soon as the underlying signal (a job change, a company change) is detected. The `updateDate` field on any record shows when it was last verified. Signal lookback defaults to 6 months, configurable via `signalsStartDate` (REST API) or `startDate` (MCP signals tools).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when a contact is not found?">
  The API returns a `200 OK` with an `error` object inside the contact payload (e.g., `"name": "EMPTY_DATA"`, `"message": "Could not find requested data"`). No credit is charged for records with no match.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is a partial profile?">
  A partial profile is a contact record that exists in Lusha's database but has incomplete data - still potentially useful as a prospect. How you opt in depends on the endpoint:

  - Single-contact enrichment endpoints use a `partialProfile: true` parameter.
  - The prospecting search-and-enrich endpoint uses `includePartialContact: true` in the request body.

<Accordion title="What does GDPR mean for the data returned?">
  Lusha applies GDPR compliance automatically. For contacts in the EU, data is returned subject to applicable regulations. Requests that cannot be fulfilled due to legal reasons return `451 Unavailable for Legal Reasons`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the Do Not Call (DNC) flag?">
  Phone numbers marked `doNotCall: true` are on the Do Not Call registry. The `excludeDnc` parameter (Scale plan) filters out contacts whose phone numbers are all DNC, so only contacts with at least one callable number appear in results.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is Waterfall Reveal, and how does it help with data coverage?">
  Waterfall Reveal is an enhancement to Enrich Contacts. When Lusha's own data has no match for a field, it falls through to your enabled third-party providers to try to fill the gap - extra reach on hard-to-match contacts, on top of standard enrichment.

  How it works:
  - If Data Waterfall is enabled on your account, with specific providers turned on under **Account > Waterfall** in the Lusha dashboard, the waterfall runs automatically on every Enrich Contacts call - you don't need to pass anything to trigger it.
  - Pass `waterfallEnabled: false` to opt a specific call out.
  - `reveal` still controls **which fields** come back; `waterfallEnabled` only controls whether the fallback runs for this call at all.
  - **Requires Data Waterfall enabled on your account** - if it's off, or no providers are enabled, `waterfallEnabled` has no effect either way.
  - Lusha determines the provider query order - it isn't caller-configurable.
  - Use of Data Waterfall is subject to Lusha's Supplementary Terms.

  ```json
  {
    "ids": ["4389064654", "4389064624"],
    "reveal": ["emails", "phones"],
    "waterfallEnabled": false
  }
  ```
</Accordion>
