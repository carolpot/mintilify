Buying Group answers the question that follows "which accounts should I work?" - namely, *who* inside each account you need to reach. Give it a list of companies and it returns the relevant contacts at each one, labelled with the role they're likely to play in the purchase.

Rather than working one contact at a time, you get the committee: the person who signs off, the person who champions you internally, and the people who will actually use the product.

## What it does

For each company you supply, Buying Group:

1. Resolves the company to a Lusha company record (by `domain` or `id`).
2. Scores the relevant contacts at that company.
3. Labels each contact with a persona role and returns them grouped per company.

## Persona roles

Every returned contact carries exactly one persona:

| Persona | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `decision_maker` | Has budget or sign-off authority |
| `potential_champion` | Likely internal advocate for the purchase |
| `end_user` | Likely day-to-day user of the product |

Pass `personas` to filter to specific roles, or omit it to get all three.

## Request basics

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `companies` | **Required.** Up to 25 companies, each identified by `domain` or Lusha company `id`. |
| `personas` | Optional. Filter to specific persona roles. Omit for all three. |
| `contactsLimit` | Optional. Caps contacts returned per company. Defaults to 60. |
| `pagination` | Optional. Defaults to page 0, size 100. |

You can attach your own `clientReferenceId` to each company to match results back to your records without depending on Lusha's resolved ID.

## Previews and revealing

Results are lightweight **previews** grouped by company. Each contact includes:

- `has` - the data points already visible on the contact
- `canReveal` - what you can unlock, and what it costs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Map the committee">
    Call `POST /v3/contacts/buying-group` with your target companies to get personas and previews back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reveal the people you want to reach">
    Pass the returned contact `id` to [Enrich Contacts](/enrichment/enrich-contacts) to reveal emails and phone numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Billing.** You're charged per contact returned, via the `buyingGroupContact` action. Revealing emails and phone numbers through Enrich Contacts is charged separately.
</Note>
