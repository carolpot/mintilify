---

Deep Intelligence pairs your data with AI to deliver recommendations, buying committee identification, and predictive scores tailored to your ICP - three recommendation engines, a Buying Committee suite, and three predictive models, all available through the v3 API.

<Warning>
  Coverage figures marked with * are not yet published by the data team; treat those numbers as placeholders, not confirmed figures.
</Warning>

## Recommendation engines

| Feature | Description | Coverage (Global) | Platform | Endpoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Recommendations** | AI-driven contact and company recommendations tailored to your ICP. Refreshes as your usage patterns evolve — the more you engage, the sharper the recommendations get. | 86% | API, MCP, WS Enrich | `POST /v3/contacts/lookalike`<br/>`POST /v3/companies/lookalike` |
| **ICP Hub** | Ideal Customer Profile matching and scoring hub. Consolidates fit signals into a single workspace and ranks accounts by likelihood-to-buy so reps work the right list first. | 82% | WS Enrich<br/>*Planned:* API, MCP, WS Search | Dashboard only |
| **Lookalikes** | AI-powered discovery of contacts and companies similar to your best existing customers. Returns paginated results that can be piped directly into Enrich for full data. | 88% | API, MCP, WS Enrich<br/>*Planned:* WS Search, API Search | `POST /v3/contacts/lookalike`<br/>`POST /v3/companies/lookalike` |

## Buying Committee

| Feature | Description | Coverage (Global) | Platform | Endpoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Buying Committee — End User** | AI-identified end users within a target account — the people who will actually use your product day to day. | 86%* | API, MCP, WS Enrich | `POST /v3/contacts/buying-committee` (beta) |
| **Buying Committee — Decision Makers** | AI-identified decision makers within a target account — the people with budget authority or sign-off power. | 86%* | API, MCP, WS Enrich | `POST /v3/contacts/buying-committee` (beta) |
| **Buying Committee — Potential Champion** | AI-identified potential champions within a target account — internal advocates likely to support your deal. | 86%* | API, MCP, WS Enrich | `POST /v3/contacts/buying-committee` (beta) |

## Predictive models

| Feature | Description | Coverage (Global) | Platform | Endpoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Website Visitors** | Intent signal derived from website visit activity matched to known companies in our identity graph. Surface accounts showing interest before they fill out a form. | 74% | API, MCP, WS Enrich<br/>*Planned:* WS Search | `POST /v3/companies/website-visits` (beta) |
| **Predictive Scoring (TOF/MOF)** | Top-of-funnel and mid-of-funnel likelihood-to-convert scores based on historical conversion patterns from the Lusha network and your own pipeline data. | 68% | WS Enrich<br/>*Planned:* API, MCP | Dashboard only |
| **Scoring — Signal** | Composite score derived from real-time buying signals — headcount changes, hiring surges, news events, and more — ranking accounts by signal strength. | 86%* | API, MCP, WS Enrich | `POST /v3/companies/scoring/signal` (beta) |
