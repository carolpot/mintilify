Deep Intelligence pairs your data with AI to deliver recommendations, buying committee identification, and predictive scores tailored to your ICP - three recommendation engines, a Buying Committee suite, and three predictive models, all available through the v3 API.

<Warning>
  Coverage figures for **Scoring - Signal** and the three **Buying Committee** features are not yet published by the data team; treat those numbers as placeholders, not confirmed figures.
</Warning>

## Recommendation engines

### Recommendations

AI-driven contact and company recommendations tailored to your ICP. Refreshes as your usage patterns evolve — the more you engage, the sharper the recommendations get.

**Coverage:** Global 86% &middot; NA 94% &middot; EMEA 82% &middot; APAC 68% - Records we can recommend with high confidence based on your seed list and recent engagement signals.

**Platform availability:** Live: API, MCP, WS Enrich

**Available via:** `POST /v3/contacts/lookalike`, `POST /v3/companies/lookalike`

### ICP Hub

Ideal Customer Profile matching and scoring hub. Consolidates fit signals into a single workspace and ranks accounts by likelihood-to-buy so reps work the right list first.

**Coverage:** Global 82% &middot; NA 91% &middot; EMEA 78% &middot; APAC 62% - Accounts where we have enough firmographic and contact data to score against your ICP criteria.

**Platform availability:** Live: WS Enrich; Planned: API, MCP, WS Search

**Available via:** Not yet available via API - currently surfaced in the Lusha dashboard only.

### Lookalikes

AI-powered discovery of contacts and companies similar to your best existing customers. Returns paginated results that can be piped directly into Enrich for full data.

**Coverage:** Global 88% &middot; NA 95% &middot; EMEA 85% &middot; APAC 72% - Records where similarity matching returns at least 100 high-confidence matches against your seed.

**Platform availability:** Live: API, MCP, WS Enrich; Planned: WS Search, API Search

**Available via:** `POST /v3/contacts/lookalike`, `POST /v3/companies/lookalike`

## Predictive models

### Website Visitors

Intent signal derived from website visit activity matched to known companies in our identity graph. Surface accounts showing interest before they fill out a form.

**Coverage:** Global 74% &middot; NA 86% &middot; EMEA 70% &middot; APAC 52% - Visitor-to-company resolution rate from anonymous web traffic on enrolled domains.

**Platform availability:** Live: API, MCP, WS Enrich; Planned: WS Search

**Available via:** `POST /v3/companies/website-visits` (beta)

### Predictive Scoring (TOF/MOF)

Top-of-funnel and mid-of-funnel likelihood-to-convert scores based on historical conversion patterns from the Lusha network and your own pipeline data.

**Coverage:** Global 68% &middot; NA 82% &middot; EMEA 64% &middot; APAC 45% - Records where we have enough conversion signal to assign a confident TOF or MOF score.

**Platform availability:** Live: WS Enrich; Planned: API, MCP

**Available via:** Not yet available via API - currently surfaced in the Lusha dashboard only.

### Scoring — Signal

Composite score derived from real-time buying signals — headcount changes, hiring surges, news events, and more — ranking accounts by signal strength.

**Coverage:** Global 86% &middot; NA 94% &middot; EMEA 82% &middot; APAC 68% - Coverage data not yet published for this feature; confirm with the data team before relying on these figures.

**Platform availability:** Live: API, MCP, WS Enrich

**Available via:** `POST /v3/companies/scoring/signal` (beta)
