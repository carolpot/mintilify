## Recommendation engines

<CardGroup cols={1}>

<Card title="Recommendations" icon="lightbulb" href="/user-guide/ai-recommendations/how-ai-recommendations-work">
  AI-driven contact and company recommendations tailored to your ICP. Refreshes as your usage patterns evolve — the more you engage, the sharper the recommendations get.

  **Global coverage:** 86% — records we can recommend with high confidence based on your seed list and recent engagement signals.

  **Endpoints:** `POST /v3/contacts/lookalike` · `POST /v3/companies/lookalike`
</Card>

<Card title="ICP Hub" icon="bullseye" href="/user-guide/ai-recommendations/icp-hub-setup-scoring-and-recommendations">
  Ideal Customer Profile matching and scoring hub. Consolidates fit signals into a single workspace and ranks accounts by likelihood-to-buy so reps work the right list first.

  **Global coverage:** 82% — accounts where we have enough firmographic and contact data to score against your ICP criteria.
</Card>

<Card title="Lookalikes" icon="users" href="/lookalikes/overview">
  AI-powered discovery of contacts and companies similar to your best existing customers. Returns paginated results that can be piped directly into Enrich for full data.

  **Global coverage:** 88% — records where similarity matching returns at least 100 high-confidence matches against your seed.

  **Endpoints:** `POST /v3/contacts/lookalike` · `POST /v3/companies/lookalike`
</Card>

</CardGroup>

## Predictive models

<CardGroup cols={1}>

<Card title="Website Visitors" icon="globe" href="/website-visits/overview">
  Intent signal derived from website visit activity matched to known companies in our identity graph. Surface accounts showing interest before they fill out a form.

  **Global coverage:** 74% — visitor-to-company resolution rate from anonymous web traffic on enrolled domains.

  **Endpoints:** `POST /v3/companies/website-visits` <span style={{ background: "#FEF3E0", color: "#92400E", borderRadius: "5px", padding: "2px 8px", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 600, marginLeft: "6px" }}>BETA</span>
</Card>

<Card title="Predictive Scoring (TOF/MOF)" icon="chart-line">
  Top-of-funnel and mid-of-funnel likelihood-to-convert scores based on historical conversion patterns from the Lusha network and your own pipeline data.

  **Global coverage:** 68% — records where we have enough conversion signal to assign a confident TOF or MOF score.
</Card>

</CardGroup>
