title: "Signal types"
description: "What each Lusha signal means, how it is detected, and how long the lookback window is."
---

Signals are real-world changes detected for contacts and companies. They are available via the [Signals API](/signals/overview) and [Webhooks](/webhooks/overview).

## Contact signals

| Signal | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| All signals | `allSignals` | Returns all available signal types for the contact |
| Promotion | `promotion` | The contact changed roles at the same company |
| Company change | `companyChange` | The contact moved to a new employer |

## Company signals

| Signal | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Website traffic increase | `websiteTrafficIncrease` | The company's website traffic increased significantly |
| Website traffic decrease | `websiteTrafficDecrease` | The company's website traffic decreased significantly |
| IT spend increase | `itSpendIncrease` | The company's IT spending increased |
| IT spend decrease | `itSpendDecrease` | The company's IT spending decreased |
| Surge in hiring | `surgeInHiring` | The company shows a surge in overall hiring activity |
| Surge in hiring by department | `surgeInHiringByDepartment` | The company shows a surge in hiring for a specific department |
| Surge in hiring by location | `surgeInHiringByLocation` | The company shows a surge in hiring in a specific location |
| Headcount increase (1/3/6/12 month) | `headcountIncrease1m` / `3m` / `6m` / `12m` | The company's headcount grew over the given window |
| Headcount decrease (1/3/6/12 month) | `headcountDecrease1m` / `3m` / `6m` / `12m` | The company's headcount shrank over the given window |
| Risk news | `riskNews` | Security issue, lawsuit faced |
| Commercial activity news | `commercialActivityNews` | Partnership, new customer, new vendor |
| Corporate strategy news | `corporateStrategyNews` | M&A, facilities expansion, new location, facility closure, asset sale, lawsuit filed |
| Financial events news | `financialEventsNews` | Funding round, asset investment, strategic investment, IPO |
| People news | `peopleNews` | Executive hire, executive departure, executive promotion, headcount increase, headcount decrease |
| Market intelligence news | `marketIntelligenceNews` | Event participation, recognition, competitor activity |
| Product activity news | `productActivityNews` | Product launch, product development, product integration |

<Note>
  The seven `*News` signal types each return the same field shape: `eventType` (the specific sub-event, e.g. `"Security Issue"`), `eventCategory` (the broad grouping shown above), `eventSummary` (a short AI-generated summary), `eventEffectiveDate`, `articleTitle`, `articleSentence`, `articleUrl`, and `articlePublishedDate`.
</Note>

Company signals also support sub-filters — `newsEventTypes`, `hiringByDepartments`, and `hiringByLocations` — to narrow results to specific sub-event types or departments/locations without a separate request.

## Lookback window

By default, signals are returned for the past **6 months**. Use the `signalsStartDate` parameter (REST API, `YYYY-MM-DD`) or `startDate` (MCP signals tools) to extend or restrict the window. `maxResultsPerSignal` caps how many results are returned per signal type per entity.
