  **Beta.** Decision Makers is a new endpoint. Its request and response shape may change before general availability.
</Warning>

Decision Makers answers a common prospecting question directly: "who should I talk to at this company?" Give it a list of companies — by domain or Lusha company ID — and it returns the most relevant contacts at each one, ranked by Lusha's buying-committee-ranker model.

## What it does

For each company you supply, Decision Makers:

1. Resolves the company to a Lusha company record (by `domain` or `id`).
2. Ranks the most relevant decision makers at that company.
3. Returns them grouped per company, in ranked order.

Results are **free previews** — name, title, company, location, LinkedIn, seniority, and department — with `has` and `canReveal` fields telling you what's already visible and what you can unlock. Decision Makers does not reveal emails or phone numbers itself.

## How to identify companies

Each entry in `companies` needs exactly one identifier:

| Identifier | Description |
|---|---|
| `domain` | Resolved to a Lusha company ID server-side. Example: `"google.com"` |
| `id` | Lusha company ID — encrypted format (`v1.…`) or legacy numeric ID, accepted during the migration window. |

You can also attach your own `clientReferenceId` to each company so you can match results back to your records without depending on Lusha's resolved ID.

## Pairing with Enrich Contacts

Decision Makers and Enrich Contacts are designed to be used together:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the decision makers">
    Call `POST /v3/contacts/decision-makers` with your target companies. You get back ranked contact previews with `has` (fields already visible) and `canReveal` (fields you can unlock, and their credit cost).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reveal contact details">
    For the contacts you want to reach out to, call [Enrich Contacts](/enrichment/enrich-contacts) with the returned contact `id` to reveal emails and phone numbers. Only the reveal itself is charged — previewing decision makers doesn't spend your email/phone credits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Because previews are free, it's cheap to run Decision Makers across a large target account list and only pay to reveal contact details for the companies and people that matter most.
</Tip>

## Pagination

Use `pagination.page` and `pagination.size` to page through decision makers per company (`size` accepts 1–100, default 10).
