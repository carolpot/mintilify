Lusha enrichments let you add professional and firmographic data to existing rows in your Clay tables. By mapping an identifier like a LinkedIn URL, email, or company domain, you can automatically pull in phone numbers, emails, and growth signals without leaving Clay.

**Prerequisites:** An active Clay account and a Lusha API key for contact and company enrichments. Your API key is available at [dashboard.lusha.com/enrich/api](https://dashboard.lusha.com/api) (visible to Admins and Managers only). For full API reference, see [docs.lusha.com](https://docs.lusha.com).

---

## How to add a Lusha enrichment to a Clay table

1. Open your table in Clay and click **+ Add column** in the toolbar.
2. Type "Lusha" in the search bar of the tools panel.
3. Select the enrichment you want to use (e.g., **Enrich person** or **Find company news signal**).
4. Map the required input field from your table - such as **Email**, **LinkedIn URL**, or **Company Domain**.
5. Click **Save and Run** to start enriching your rows.

---

## What each enrichment returns

- **Person enrichments** - job titles, seniority, departments, and verified contact details
- **Company enrichments** - employee count, founded year, company type, and firmographics
- **Signal enrichments** - website traffic trends, IT spend estimates, headcount growth, and recent news

<Note>
**Enrich person** and **Enrich company** deduct from your Lusha plan credits. All signal enrichments and Lookalike sources use Clay credits.
</Note>

---

## Tips for scaling

- **Test first** - run the enrichment on 10-20 rows before processing your full table to validate output and credit cost
- **Check balances** - view Lusha credits in your Lusha account; view Clay credits in the top-right of the Clay interface
- **Use filters** - apply table filters to run enrichments only on rows that meet your criteria and save credits
