Search and Enrich Companies combines [Search Companies](/enrichment/search-companies) and [Enrich Companies](/enrichment/enrich-companies) into one request. Send the same identifiers you'd use for Search Companies, and get back fully enriched firmographic profiles - no intermediate step to inspect matches and pass `id` values around.

<Info>
  This is a single endpoint for one company or a hundred. Send an array of 1 to 100 companies in one call.
</Info>

## When to use this instead of the two-step flow

Use Search and Enrich Companies when you already trust your match criteria and want the full firmographic record in one round trip - for example, enriching every account in a target-account list you've already vetted by domain.

Use the two-step [Search](/enrichment/search-companies) → [Enrich](/enrichment/enrich-companies) flow instead when you want to inspect `canReveal` and its credit cost before spending credits, or when you need to add specific breakdowns like `competitors` or `intent` that this combined endpoint doesn't expose as a `reveal` option.

## Identifiers you can search by

Provide **at least one** of the following for each company:

- Lusha company `id`
- `name`
- `domain`

## What you get back

Every matched company returns full firmographics automatically - description, company type, year founded, employee count, industry and sub-industry, locations, social links, revenue range, funding history, technologies, and business model. Unlike Enrich Companies, this endpoint doesn't take a `reveal` field for employee breakdowns, competitors, or intent - use the two-step flow if you need those.

If a company can't be found or enriched, its result contains an `error` with a code of `NOT_FOUND`, `COMPLIANCE_RESTRICTED`, or `ENRICH_FAILED` instead of profile data.

## Billing

Billed the same as Enrich Companies - charged per successful result via the `reveal_company` action.
