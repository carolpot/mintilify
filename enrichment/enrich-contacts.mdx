Enrich Contacts reveals emails and phone numbers for contacts you've already found with [Search Contacts](/enrichment/search-contacts). It only accepts Lusha contact `id` values - not emails, LinkedIn URLs, or names - so you always search first, inspect what's available, and enrich only the contacts you actually want to spend credits on.

<Info>
  This is a single endpoint for one contact or a hundred. Send an array of 1 to 100 `id` values in one call.
</Info>

## What you can reveal

Use the `reveal` field to control what gets unlocked for each contact:

- `emails` - work and personal email addresses
- `phones` - mobile and direct phone numbers

Omit `reveal` entirely to get both by default.

<Tip>
  Check each contact's `canReveal` array from your Search Contacts response before enriching - it tells you exactly which fields are available and how many credits each one costs, so you don't spend credits enriching a contact with no phone number on file.
</Tip>

## What you get back

Each enriched contact includes full name, job title, seniority, department, location (with EU flag and coordinates), company reference, social links, previous employment history, and any tags you've applied in Lusha - in addition to the revealed emails and phones.

If enrichment fails for a contact `id`, the result contains an `error` with a code of `NOT_FOUND`, `COMPLIANCE_RESTRICTED`, or `ENRICH_FAILED` instead of profile data.

## Billing

Enrich Contacts is charged per revealed field (email or phone) via per-datapoint pricing - not per contact. A contact with two revealed emails and one revealed phone is billed for three data points.
