Enrich Contacts reveals emails and phone numbers for contacts you've already found with [Search Contacts](/enrichment/search-contacts). It only accepts Lusha contact `id` values - not emails, LinkedIn URLs, or names - so you always search first, inspect what's available, and enrich only the contacts you actually want to spend credits on.

<Info>
  This is a single endpoint for one contact or a hundred. Send an array of 1 to 100 `id` values in one call.
</Info>

## What you can reveal

Use the `reveal` field to control what gets unlocked for each contact:

- `emails` - work and personal email addresses
- `phones` - mobile and direct phone numbers

Omit `reveal` entirely to get both by default.

<Tip>
  Check each contact's `canReveal` array from your Search Contacts response before enriching - it tells you exactly which fields are available and how many credits each one costs, so you don't spend credits enriching a contact with no phone number on file.
</Tip>

## What you get back

Each enriched contact includes full name, job title, seniority, department, location (with EU flag and coordinates), company reference, social links, previous employment history, and any tags you've applied in Lusha - in addition to the revealed emails and phones.

If enrichment fails for a contact `id`, the result contains an `error` with a code of `NOT_FOUND`, `COMPLIANCE_RESTRICTED`, or `ENRICH_FAILED` instead of profile data.

## Data Waterfall and async results

If [Data Waterfall](/user-guide/lushas-api/data-waterfall) is enabled on your account, a requested email or phone that Lusha doesn't have can be filled by your connected external providers. Pass `waterfallEnabled: false` to keep a call to Lusha data only.

When the waterfall runs, the response also includes:

- `status` and `statusDescription` - the outcome of the request. Branch on `status`, never on `statusDescription`.
- `statusReason` - why a requested field is empty when the waterfall didn't run: `WATERFALL_NOT_CONFIGURED`, `WATERFALL_NOT_IN_PLAN`, or `WATERFALL_DISABLED`.
- `dataSource` on each email and phone - `lusha`, or `<providerId>` (for example `wiza`). Vendor-delivered data points also carry `credits`.
- `missingDataPoints` on a contact - requested data points still unresolved, with a status of `NOT_FOUND` or `BILLING_FAILED`.

These fields are omitted when the waterfall didn't run, so a Lusha-only response keeps its usual shape.

### Polling a job

Fast providers answer inside the Enrich Contacts call. A slower provider doesn't hold the request open. Instead, the response returns what's already known, plus a `job`:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check for a job">
    If the response has no `job`, it's final. You're done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait, then poll">
    Wait `job.retryAfter` seconds, then call [`GET /v3/contacts/jobs/{jobId}`](/api-reference/enrich/get-contacts-enrich-job) with `job.id`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep polling while processing">
    While the job `status` is `processing`, wait `retryAfter` seconds and poll again. `covers` lists what's still outstanding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merge or stop">
    On `complete`, merge the job's `contacts` into your enrich results by `id`. The job returns only the contacts it was still working on, so don't replace the whole enrich payload. On `expired` or `failed`, stop and keep what Enrich Contacts already returned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Branch on whether `job` is present, and then on the job `status`. Don't infer that work is still running because an email or phone is empty.
</Warning>

A `404` from the poll means the job doesn't exist, has expired, or belongs to another account - stop polling. A `503` means the job store couldn't be reached - retry, since the job isn't gone.

Only Enrich Contacts returns a `job`. [Search and Enrich Contacts](/enrichment/search-and-enrich-contacts) returns the same waterfall fields, but never a `job`, so there's nothing to poll.

## Billing

Enrich Contacts is charged per revealed field (email or phone) via per-datapoint pricing - not per contact. A contact with two revealed emails and one revealed phone is billed for three data points.

Polling a job is free. Each data point is billed once, by whichever call delivered it: `billing.creditsCharged` on Enrich Contacts is the synchronous charge, and `creditsCharged` on the job is the async charge only.
