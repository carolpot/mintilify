Enrich Companies reveals full firmographic data for companies you've already found with [Search Companies](/enrichment/search-companies). It only accepts Lusha company `id` values, so you always search first and enrich only the companies you actually want to spend credits on.

<Info>
  This is a single endpoint for one company or a hundred. Send an array of 1 to 100 `id` values in one call.
</Info>

## What you get by default

Every enriched company includes firmographics - description, company type, year founded, employee count, industry and sub-industry, SIC and NAICS codes, HQ and additional locations, social links, revenue range, funding history, technologies, and business model.

## What you can add with `reveal`

Pass any of these to add deeper breakdowns to the response:

- `employeesByDepartment` - headcount by department
- `employeesByLocation` - headcount by country and state
- `employeesBySeniority` - headcount by seniority level
- `competitors` - companies Lusha identifies as competitors
- `intent` - buyer intent topics

<Tip>
  Check each company's `canReveal` array from your Search Companies response before enriching to confirm the credit cost.
</Tip>

If enrichment fails for a company `id`, the result contains an `error` with a code of `NOT_FOUND`, `COMPLIANCE_RESTRICTED`, or `ENRICH_FAILED` instead of profile data.

## Billing

Enrich Companies is charged per successful result via the `reveal_company` action - one charge per company, regardless of how many `reveal` fields you request for it.
